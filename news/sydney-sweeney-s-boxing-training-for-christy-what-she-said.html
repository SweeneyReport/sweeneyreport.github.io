Sydney Sweeney's Real Boxing Training for Christy, Explained

Sydney Sweeney told W Magazine about her roughly three-and-a-half-month training routine to play boxer Christy Martin in the biopic Christy.

By Sweeney Report · November 14, 2026

Sydney Sweeney prepared for the biopic Christy with a demanding daily schedule of weightlifting and kickboxing. According to her W Magazine interview, she had about three and a half months of preparation before playing boxer Christy Martin. Good Morning America, which covered the interview, reported that she also gained about 30 pounds for the part.

The daily routine

Sweeney told W Magazine that her day started with about an hour of weight training in the morning. Around midday she did roughly two hours of kickboxing. In the evening she returned to the weights for another hour. She described the result as feeling "crazy strong," according to Good Morning America's account of the interview.

A background in martial arts

Sweeney also said the sport was not entirely new to her. Per Good Morning America's coverage, she said her parents enrolled her in grappling and kickboxing, and that she trained at a dojo from about age 13 to 19. She said she was the only girl there and sparred with the boys.

The film and its reception

Christy was directed by David Michôd and also stars Ben Foster, Merritt Wever and Katy O'Brian. Wikipedia's entry on the film says it was shot in North Carolina in fall 2024, premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival and opened in U.S. theaters on November 7, 2025, through Black Bear Pictures. The same entry says Sweeney performed her own boxing stunts. Michôd praised her commitment, saying, per Good Morning America, that she arrived on set ready to work every day.

Reviews were mixed overall. Wikipedia's summary of review aggregators lists a 66% score on Rotten Tomatoes and 58 out of 100 on Metacritic, while critics widely singled out Sweeney's performance for praise. Commercially, the film struggled. Wikipedia reports an opening of about $1.3 million in the U.S. and a total gross of roughly $2.3 million against a $15 million budget.

What's next

Sweeney appeared alongside the real Christy Martin at the International Boxing Hall of Fame induction ceremony on June 8, 2025, according to Good Morning America. With the film's theatrical run largely behind it, attention is likely to turn to how the performance fares during awards season and on streaming, and to which physical transformation Sweeney takes on next.

Sydney Sweeney's The Voyeurs: How Critics Really Rated It

Sydney Sweeney's Prime Video thriller The Voyeurs drew mixed reviews, with Rotten Tomatoes under 50% and a 54 on Metacritic.

By Sweeney Report · November 17, 2026

The Voyeurs, the Amazon thriller starring Sydney Sweeney, divided critics when it reached Prime Video on September 10, 2021. According to Wikipedia's summary of review aggregators, the film holds a 45% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes, based on 38 reviews. Metacritic gives it 54 out of 100, a score it labels "mixed or average." It was an early lead role for Sweeney.

The background

Michael Mohan wrote and directed the film, and Justice Smith played opposite Sweeney. Ben Hardy, Natasha Liu Bordizzo and others rounded out the cast. Divide/Conquer produced the film, and Amazon Studios distributed it. Principal photography reportedly began on October 29, 2019, and wrapped in early December in Montreal. The story follows a young couple who become fixated on watching their neighbors, with dangerous consequences.

How critics reacted

Reviews varied widely. Slant called the film audaciously trashy, while The Playlist described it as sluggish and predictable. Variety's Nick Schager said it felt like recycled material made for younger viewers who might not know classics such as Rear Window. Richard Roeper of the Chicago Sun-Times wrote that the story held his attention for a while before it became wildly implausible. The San Francisco Chronicle's G. Allen Johnson called the script pedestrian.

Not every critic was negative. The Guardian's Charles Bramesco gave the film four stars out of five and described it as funny, diabolical and perverted, which made it one of the more enthusiastic reviews.

Why it still matters

The Voyeurs came out as Sweeney's profile was growing, and it gave her a leading part in a studio-backed streaming release. The mixed reception left the film open to debate among viewers, and it remains a talking point in discussions of her early filmography.

What's next: Viewers can still judge the film for themselves on Prime Video, where it premiered. Do you think The Voyeurs is underrated or overrated?

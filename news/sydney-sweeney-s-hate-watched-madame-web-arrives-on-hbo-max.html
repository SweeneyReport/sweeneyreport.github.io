Sydney Sweeney's Most Hate-Watched Movie Just Hit HBO Max

Madame Web, the Sony Spider-Man spinoff with Sydney Sweeney as Julia Cornwall, has officially landed on HBO Max, IMDb reported.

By Sweeney Report · November 15, 2026

Madame Web, the Sony Spider-Man spinoff that features Sydney Sweeney, has officially arrived on HBO Max. IMDb reported that the film, which it described as Sweeney's most "hate-watched" movie, is now available to stream on the service. Dakota Johnson leads the cast, and Sweeney plays Julia Cornwall, one of the young heroes at the center of the story.

The background

Madame Web opened in theaters in February 2024 as part of Sony's run of films set around Spider-Man characters. Johnson headlines the movie, while Sweeney appears in an ensemble of younger characters who are drawn into the story's superhero plot.

The film reportedly grossed about $100 million worldwide against a production budget of around $80 million. Those figures are approximate and have been reported rather than confirmed in detail, and they do not include marketing costs. Even so, the numbers have often been part of the conversation around how the movie performed compared with other superhero releases.

Why it keeps coming up

IMDb's headline framed the movie as a "hate-watched" title, a label for films that viewers seek out in large part because of their poor reputation. That description is IMDb's characterization, and opinions on the film vary from viewer to viewer.

Since its release, Madame Web has remained a frequent topic of online discussion. Its streaming debut on HBO Max gives audiences who skipped it in theaters a new, easy way to judge it for themselves. It also gives longtime critics a chance to revisit their views, and some viewers may come away with a different opinion than the one they expected.

Sweeney's role

For Sweeney, the character of Julia Cornwall was one of several high-profile projects in a busy stretch of her career. Her turn in the film has been discussed alongside the movie's wider reception, though the picture as a whole has drawn far more attention than any single performance.

What's next

With Madame Web now on HBO Max, viewers can settle the debate for themselves: is it a secretly fun watch, or as weak as its reputation suggests? Sweeney's other projects and business ventures continue to draw attention, and the Sweeney Report will keep tracking the latest news as it develops.

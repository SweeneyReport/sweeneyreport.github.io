Sydney Sweeney's $220M Thriller Won't Leave Streaming Charts

ScreenRant reported that The Housemaid and Anyone but You, both R-rated, sat on HBO Max's chart above Sydney Sweeney's other theatrical releases.

By Sweeney Report · October 28, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's R-rated psychological thriller The Housemaid, which earned about $220 million at the box office, was still showing up on HBO Max's streaming chart in late October 2026. ScreenRant reported that the film is one of two R-rated Sweeney movies on the chart. The other is the romantic comedy Anyone but You. According to the outlet, both have risen above her other theatrical releases on the service.

A chart presence for two R-rated films

ScreenRant's Felipe Rangel wrote on October 27 that the two titles stood out among Sweeney's big-screen work. The outlet said one of them had also broken into the streamer's top 10. It described Sweeney as one of the most in-demand actresses working today.

ScreenRant also pointed to the range of her credits. They include the HBO series Euphoria, The White Lotus and Sharp Objects, plus the films Immaculate and Christy. It said her move to the big screen had been very lucrative for both her and the studios.

ComingSoon.net also covered the streaming run. Its headline said Anyone but You was suddenly back on HBO Max's top 10 in the US.

About the film

The Housemaid is based on the novel of the same name by Freida McFadden. Amanda Seyfried also stars. ScreenRant noted that Anyone but You is loosely based on a William Shakespeare play.

IMDb described The Housemaid as one of the biggest streaming hits and noted that a sequel is on the way. The sources reviewed did not give a release date or plot details for the follow-up.

Why it matters

A theatrical hit that keeps finding viewers on streaming extends a film's reach well beyond its cinema run. For Sweeney, having two adult-oriented titles ahead of her other theatrical releases on HBO Max, according to ScreenRant, suggests her audience is following her across genres. One is a thriller and the other a rom-com.

What's next: The sequel to The Housemaid is the main project to watch, since IMDb reported it was in the works. Whether it can match the original's $220 million box office and its streaming performance is still an open question.

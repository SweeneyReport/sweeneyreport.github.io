Sydney Sweeney's Best-Reviewed Performance, According to Critics

Reality, the 2023 HBO film in which Sydney Sweeney played Reality Winner, holds a 93% Rotten Tomatoes score, the highest of her career.

By Sweeney Report · November 21, 2026

Of all of Sydney Sweeney's screen credits, one stands highest with critics: Reality, the 2023 HBO Films drama in which she played former NSA contractor Reality Winner. According to Rotten Tomatoes, the film holds a 93 percent score based on 92 reviews, and the site lists it as the top-rated title on her page.

The film behind the score

Reality was directed by Tina Satter, who co-wrote the script with James Paul Dallas. It is based on the transcript of the FBI's interrogation of Winner. The film premiered at the Berlin International Film Festival on February 18, 2023, and reached HBO on May 29, 2023. It also received theatrical releases in the UK, Ireland, Australia and France. Josh Hamilton and Marchánt Davis play two of the agents in the cast.

What critics said

The Rotten Tomatoes critics consensus described the film's narrow scope and gripping, fact-based story as adding up to a riveting showcase for Sweeney in the title role. On Metacritic, the film scored 83 out of 100 from 27 critics, a rating the site labels universal acclaim.

Individual outlets were just as warm. According to the film's Wikipedia entry, which collects the reviews, Rolling Stone ran a headline saying the film gave Sweeney a movie-star moment, while Empire called it her best performance yet. Another review, cited there, described a tour de force as a cornered whistleblower.

Awards attention

The performance came shortly after a breakout stretch on television. A year earlier, Sweeney earned her first two Emmy nominations, for Euphoria and The White Lotus. For Reality, she was reportedly considered for honors including a Critics' Choice nomination for best actress, and the film itself was nominated for a Gotham Award and won a Peabody Award.

Scores shift as reviews are added. Early in 2023 the film briefly showed 100 percent on only a handful of reviews, and its audience score has been far lower than the critics' number, at around two-thirds in recent snapshots.

What's next

Sweeney has continued to balance prestige projects with mainstream releases, and Reality remains the benchmark for critics. Whether a future role overtakes it is an open question, and one fans are likely to keep debating.

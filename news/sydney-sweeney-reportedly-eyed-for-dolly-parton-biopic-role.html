Sydney Sweeney Eyed for Dolly Parton Role? Fans Pick Someone Else

Sydney Sweeney is reportedly one of several names linked to a Dolly Parton biopic, and fans are divided. Nothing has been confirmed.

By Sweeney Report · October 30, 2026

Sydney Sweeney has reportedly been named as a contender to play Dolly Parton in a possible biopic. Reality Tea, citing a source who spoke to RadarOnline, reported that the Euphoria star was one of the top names under discussion. Fashion Times UK reported that the news prompted a heated reaction online, with some fans saying Sabrina Carpenter would be a better choice. No casting has been officially announced.

The reported shortlist

According to Reality Tea's account of the RadarOnline report, Sweeney was said to be competing with several other well-known performers. The names reportedly included Sabrina Carpenter, Carrie Underwood, Hilary Duff, Kaley Cuoco and Kristin Chenoweth. The source reportedly said Underwood's name kept "coming up" because her personality was seen as similar to Parton's.

The same report said Parton had once mentioned Reese Witherspoon and Scarlett Johansson as actors who could step into her shoes on screen. Reality Tea also reported that Parton died on August 25 at the age of 80, and that talk of a biopic had surfaced several times since then.

Who would decide

The source reportedly said Parton's family had been eager to bring her story to the big screen and would have the final say on who plays her. In the source's words, as quoted by Reality Tea, a well-made film featuring her best-known music would be "a massive success." Reality Tea added that Parton's last project was described as "DOLLY: A True Original Musical," an autobiographical Broadway show still set to open next January.

How fans reacted

Fashion Times UK reported that some social media users questioned whether Sweeney was the right fit. One reaction called the idea "the disrespect." Others argued that Carpenter's experience as both a singer and an actress made her a stronger option. The outlet noted that Carpenter had paid tribute to Parton at a Country Music Association celebration, remembering her as a symbol of joy, kindness, light and love.

Some commenters asked whether another film about Parton was needed at all. They suggested audiences could keep enjoying her existing music and screen appearances. Fashion Times UK stressed that these remarks were individual opinions and not facts about any actor's abilities. It also said there was no evidence that Sweeney, Carpenter or anyone else had been approached, auditioned or offered the part.

What's next

For now, the story rests on a single anonymous source relayed through RadarOnline. No studio, filmmaker or representative for Sweeney has commented in the reports reviewed. Any real casting would reportedly depend on Parton's family and the team behind the project. Until an official announcement arrives, the lead role remains an open question.

Sydney Sweeney Reportedly Eyeing a Secret Move to England

The Sun reportedly says Sydney Sweeney viewed a home in Little Tew and is weighing a part-time move to England with Scooter Braun.

By Sweeney Report · November 8, 2026

Sydney Sweeney is reportedly considering a quiet move to England. The Sun reported, citing an unnamed source, that the actor viewed a home in Little Tew, a village in the Cotswolds area of West Oxfordshire. She is reportedly weighing the decision with her boyfriend, Scooter Braun. Neither Sweeney nor Braun has publicly confirmed the report.

What the report says

According to The Sun, the couple could divide their time between the United States and England rather than relocating permanently. The newspaper's source reportedly said Sweeney has visited the Cotswolds twice over the past ten months and has enjoyed the region.

AOL, republishing coverage from The Mirror, described the move as something she was reportedly considering quietly, and it noted the property's location near Pinewood Studios. BANG Showbiz, in a version carried by Yahoo Style Canada, described the plan as a secret one. All of these accounts trace back to The Sun's single anonymous source, so the details remain unverified.

Potential neighbors

The reports also pointed to the well-known people who already have homes in the area. Those named include David and Victoria Beckham, as well as Ellen DeGeneres and her wife, Portia de Rossi. The outlets did not say that Sweeney had met any of them or that any contact was planned.

Why it matters

The Cotswolds is a popular retreat for public figures, and its proximity to major UK film studios could be practical for an actor working on international projects. That is context rather than anything reported about Sweeney's own plans, and no upcoming UK production has been tied to the story.

What's next: For now, the story rests on an unnamed source and has no confirmation from Sweeney or her representatives. Any decision to buy or rent a property would likely come out through further reporting or a statement from her team.

Sydney Sweeney Filmed a Tearful Scene Days After That Kiss

Sydney Sweeney was photographed crying on the New York set of The Housemaid's Secret, reportedly days after filming an onscreen kiss with Stephen Kalyn.

By Sweeney Report · November 8, 2026

Sydney Sweeney was photographed crying in character while shooting The Housemaid's Secret in New York on November 4, according to AOL, which republished a report from Wealth of Geeks. The scene was reportedly filmed just days after an onscreen kiss with costar Stephen Kalyn. Sweeney was working on a street sequence alongside Kalyn and Michele Morrone.

Who is back for the sequel

Sweeney is returning as Millie Calloway, the housekeeper at the center of the 2025 thriller The Housemaid. Morrone is also back in the role of Enzo, while Kalyn is described in the report as one of the new additions to the franchise. Paul Feig is directing again, and screenwriter Rebecca Sonnenshine is returning for the adaptation, AOL reported.

The story behind the film

The sequel is based on Freida McFadden's second Housemaid novel, which was published in 2023. Lionsgate announced the follow-up in January. The first film was adapted from McFadden's earlier book and also starred Amanda Seyfried, according to the same report.

The outlet did not provide details about what the tearful scene involves in the story, and the studio has not shared plot information about this part of the film. The photos show only a moment from a working day on location, so it is unclear how the sequence will appear in the finished movie. The report on the timing of the kiss scene was also not independently confirmed here, which is why it is described as reportedly happening days earlier.

Why it matters

Location photos from New York give an early look at production on a sequel that fans of the first film have been waiting to see. They also confirm that the main cast is working together on the street scenes, with Sweeney and Morrone reprising their roles and Kalyn joining them.

What's next: No release date was mentioned in the source material. Production on The Housemaid's Secret appears to be continuing, and further set photos or official updates from Lionsgate may follow.

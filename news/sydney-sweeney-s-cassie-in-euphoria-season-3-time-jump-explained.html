Sydney Sweeney: Euphoria Season 3 Explained in 30 Seconds

Euphoria returned to HBO on April 12, 2026 with a five-year time jump that left Sydney Sweeney's Cassie engaged to Nate and living in the suburbs.

By Sweeney Report · November 15, 2026

Euphoria returned to HBO on Sunday, April 12, 2026, and Sydney Sweeney's Cassie Howard was at the center of the biggest change: a time jump of roughly five years. The third season, which has eight episodes released weekly, found Cassie engaged to Nate Jacobs and living in the suburbs, while Rue was far from home in Mexico. Zendaya, Jacob Elordi and Hunter Schafer all came back, and Sharon Stone joined as a newcomer.

A five-year leap

Creator Sam Levinson explained that he picked a five-year gap because the characters would have been out of college by then, according to coverage of the season's early promotion. The story therefore did not pick up where season two ended.

TVLine reported that Cassie and Nate were engaged and living in the suburbs, and that Levinson described Cassie as very addicted to social media. Levinson also said, as relayed in press coverage, that the couple would indeed marry. Cassie's storyline reportedly involves her efforts to become famous on TikTok.

Rue, played by Zendaya, was in Mexico working off a debt to Laurie, according to TVLine. Trade coverage described the premiere as opening with her in a desert setting. TVLine also reported that Maddy works at a Hollywood talent agency and that Jules is studying art with hopes of becoming a painter.

Who returned and who did not

TVLine listed Zendaya, Hunter Schafer, Maude Apatow, Eric Dane, Alexa Demie, Colman Domingo, Jacob Elordi, Chloe Cherry and Dominic Fike among the returning cast. New names included Sharon Stone, Natasha Lyonne, Rosalía, Marshawn Lynch and Danielle Deadwyler.

Barbie Ferreira did not return. TVLine noted that she announced her exit as Kat on Instagram in August 2022.

Early reception

Reviews were reportedly mixed. One review cited by search coverage said Zendaya slipped easily back into the role of Rue, but that several other characters, Cassie and Nate in particular, felt less developed. Sweeney also attended the Los Angeles premiere alongside other members of the cast, Tribune reported.

What's next

Euphoria aired weekly through May, and the time jump has left fans debating whether Cassie's new life or Rue's situation in Mexico was the bigger surprise. For Sweeney, the season keeps her tied to the HBO series that helped build her career while she balances film and business projects.

Sydney Sweeney Name Pops Up Over Kendall Jenner's Tequila Ad

Instagram commenters linked Kendall Jenner's new 818 Tequila ad to Sydney Sweeney's Novig spot, though Jenner has not said it was inspired by it.

By Sweeney Report · November 13, 2026

Kendall Jenner's new ad for her 818 Tequila brand has prompted some social media users to bring up Sydney Sweeney. Showbiz Cheat Sheet reported on November 11, 2026, that several Instagram commenters wondered whether the spot was a playful nod to Sweeney's recent ad for the sports betting app Novig. Jenner has not commented on any inspiration behind the campaign, according to the outlet.

What the ad shows

According to Showbiz Cheat Sheet, the spot follows Jenner through an orchard while a male narrator describes the tequila as stripped down and close to its natural state, with no added flavors, no added sweeteners and nothing artificial. He says the product is "practically naked," and the ad then cuts to Jenner, who says the tequila is the thing that is naked, not her. The outlet said the joke is built around the brand's pitch of simple ingredients.

The Sweeney connection

The comparison comes from the Novig commercial, which Showbiz Cheat Sheet said recently drew controversy and was criticized by many for the way it portrayed women in sports. BuzzFeed and AOL.com also published pieces describing the Jenner ad as possible shade aimed at Sweeney, though those framings come from social media commenters rather than from anyone involved in either campaign.

Under Jenner's Instagram post, one person wrote, "Sydney Sweeney could never," according to Showbiz Cheat Sheet. Another commenter wrote, "This is what that sports ad wanted to be." Jenner's sister Khloé Kardashian reacted to the post with a series of heart-eyes emojis, the outlet said.

Jenner's brand messaging

Showbiz Cheat Sheet noted that Jenner launched 818 Tequila in 2021 and has been closely involved in its messaging and branding. It quoted Kathleen Braine, the company's chief marketing officer, as telling Forbes that Jenner has "a next-level taste level for creative" and that this has resonated with Gen Z consumers. The outlet also cited Jenner's earlier comments about her 818 Outpost event at Coachella, which she described as a setting for celebrating with tequila.

Nothing in the reporting indicates that Jenner or 818 Tequila referenced Sweeney or the Novig ad. The link appears to rest entirely on viewer speculation, and it is unconfirmed whether the overlap in themes was intentional.

What's next

What's next: Neither Jenner nor Sweeney has publicly addressed the comparison so far. Whether either camp responds may determine if the talk fades as a passing social media moment or grows into a wider conversation about how brands use provocative advertising.

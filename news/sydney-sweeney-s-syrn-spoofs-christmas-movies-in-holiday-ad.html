Sydney Sweeney's SYRN Spoofs Classic Christmas Movies

Sydney Sweeney's SYRN brand teased its first holiday shop with an Instagram ad that spoofs classic Christmas movies and previews four collections.

By Sweeney Report · October 28, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's SYRN brand has announced its first holiday shop with a teaser that parodies several classic Christmas movies. IMDb reported that the brand is preparing four seasonal collections, named Comfy, Seductress, Playful and Romantic. The campaign teaser appeared on Instagram.

A campaign built on holiday film references

According to SSBCrack, the video is a nostalgic nod to festive favorites. It reportedly draws on How the Grinch Stole Christmas, Home Alone and Corpse Bride. SSBCrack also said another segment borrows from White Christmas.

SSBCrack reported that Sweeney plays a character inspired by Cindy Lou Who, the young Whoville resident from the Grinch story. The outlet said the clip opens with a cozy, family-movie tone before a scene change moves it into a darker, gothic mood inspired by Corpse Bride. SSBCrack said the shift is meant to show the different themes of the upcoming collections.

Four collections, starting with Comfy

IMDb described the launch as SYRN's first holiday shop, with the spoof campaign serving as its introduction. SSBCrack reported that the Comfy line was due to arrive on October 27 and would come first. It said the Seductress, Playful and Romantic collections would follow, each aimed at a different mood.

Spanish outlet Marca also covered the launch on October 27. Its headlines referred to a SYRN "Hibernation Nation" collection and a knitwear range that includes matching sweaters for dogs. It is not yet clear from the material reviewed how those items fit into the four-collection plan, so the details remain to be confirmed.

Why it matters for the brand

The campaign is another sign of Sweeney's push to expand SYRN beyond its initial launches. It is built around recognizable pop-culture moments rather than a conventional product announcement, which fits the brand's playful marketing. SSBCrack framed the project as part of her growing role as both an actress and an entrepreneur in fashion.

Neither SYRN nor Sweeney had publicly detailed a full release schedule for the later collections in the reports reviewed. Release dates and prices for those lines were not included.

What's next

With the Comfy line first in the rollout, the next step is the arrival of the Seductress, Playful and Romantic collections. Fans will be watching SYRN's Instagram for release dates and for any further holiday-movie spoofs.

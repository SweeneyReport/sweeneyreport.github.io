Sydney Sweeney: Every Major Award Nomination, Checked

Sydney Sweeney has two Emmy nominations, one SAG nod and no Golden Globe nominations. Here is what her awards record shows.

By Sweeney Report · November 7, 2026

Sydney Sweeney has never been nominated for a Golden Globe, and her biggest nominations to date are two Primetime Emmy nods from 2022. According to Wikipedia's awards list for the actress, she was nominated for Euphoria and for The White Lotus that year and won neither. Her only Screen Actors Guild Awards nomination came in 2019, as part of the ensemble of The Handmaid's Tale.

The Emmy nominations

The 2022 Emmys marked her first nominations. Entertainment Tonight reported that she became a first-time double nominee. One nomination was for supporting actress in a drama series, for her role as Cassie Howard in Euphoria. The other was for supporting actress in a limited or anthology series or movie, for The White Lotus. Wikipedia's list shows no Emmy wins. The same list shows Astra TV Awards nominations for both performances that year.

The Golden Globes gap

The Golden Globes are the clearest absence on her record. Wikipedia's list shows no Golden Globe nominations or wins. When the nominations came out in December, her boxing biopic Christy was shut out, according to coverage of the announcement. NME ran a report on the omission under the headline that she had been snubbed.

The film did earn some recognition elsewhere. Wikipedia lists an Astra Film Awards nomination for best actress in a motion picture drama for Christy. It also lists a 2026 Virtuoso Award from the Santa Barbara International Film Festival for the same film.

The wins she does have

Her list of wins is short but varied. In 2024 she won a Gracie Award for best actress in a leading role in a movie made for TV, for Reality. The same year she was nominated for a Critics' Choice Television Award for that performance. Wikipedia also records a People's Choice Awards nomination for Anyone but You in 2024. Earlier wins include a Special Shout Jury Mention for acting at the 2019 Sidewalk Film Festival, for Clementine, and a shared MTV Movie & TV Award for best fight in 2022, for Euphoria.

The Wikipedia list has gaps, and some smaller awards may be missing from it. One statistics site claimed she had a 2022 Golden Globe nomination. That claim did not match the Wikipedia list or entertainment press coverage, so this article does not treat it as fact.

What's next

Her awards record is thin next to her profile, and the pace of her output suggests more chances ahead. The Gracie Award and the Santa Barbara Virtuoso Award show that critics and festivals have noticed her dramatic work, even though the big nominations have so far been rare. Which of her roles deserved a nomination most is a matter of opinion, and fans are likely to keep debating it.

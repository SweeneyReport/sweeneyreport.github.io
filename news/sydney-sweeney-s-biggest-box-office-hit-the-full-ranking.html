Sydney Sweeney's Biggest Box Office Hit Isn't What You Think

The Housemaid is Sydney Sweeney's top-grossing lead role, but a small part in a Tarantino film still holds her biggest worldwide total.

By Sweeney Report · November 5, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's highest-grossing film is not one she led. Quentin Tarantino's Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, in which she had a small supporting role, earned more than $370 million worldwide. Among her starring vehicles, The Housemaid now holds the record. SlashFilm reported that it reached about $305 million globally, passing the $220.2 million that Anyone But You earned.

How her films compare

Her wide-release box office results have varied a lot. Immaculate, the 2024 horror film in which she played Sister Cecilia and also worked as a producer, earned roughly $28 million worldwide. Madame Web, in which she played a supporting role, took in around $100 million and was widely described as a box office disappointment.

Anyone But You, her romantic comedy with Glen Powell, was the sleeper hit of that stretch. It grossed more than $220 million worldwide. SlashFilm noted that it was the first romantic comedy in six years to pass $200 million.

The Housemaid takes the lead title

The Housemaid was released in December 2025. Sweeney starred opposite Amanda Seyfried, and Paul Feig directed. SlashFilm reported on January 31, 2026 that the film had earned about $305.3 million worldwide, including roughly $116.2 million in the United States and Canada. It cited a production budget of $35 million. Other outlets citing Variety's reporting gave similar totals. Those reports said the United Kingdom, France and Latin America were among its strongest international markets.

That total makes it her most successful film as a lead or core ensemble member. SlashFilm pointed out that Once Upon a Time in Hollywood still ranks above it in raw numbers. Sweeney played Dianne "Snake" Lake in the film, and she was neither its lead nor part of its central ensemble. Reported worldwide totals for that film run higher than the figure in the video, at roughly $392 million.

Why the ranking is debatable

The answer depends on how a film is counted. By raw worldwide gross, Tarantino's film comes first. By the size of her role and her place on the poster, The Housemaid leads, followed by Anyone But You. Sweeney also produced or executive produced some of these projects, which adds to the case that her recent hits reflect her own choices as well as her casting.

What's next

SlashFilm reported that a sequel, The Housemaid 2, was in development at Lionsgate, with Sweeney and Feig expected to return. Studio confirmation of details such as a release date had not been reported at the time of writing.

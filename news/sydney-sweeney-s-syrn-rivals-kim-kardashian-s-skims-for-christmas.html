Sydney Sweeney's SYRN Takes On Kim Kardashian's SKIMS This Christmas

Sydney Sweeney's SYRN and Kim Kardashian's SKIMS launched rival holiday campaigns, one built around The Grinch and the other around the Kardashian-Jenner family.

By Sweeney Report · November 13, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's lingerie brand SYRN and Kim Kardashian's SKIMS both unveiled Christmas campaigns this month, setting up a seasonal face-off between the two labels. According to a Hello! article republished by Yahoo on November 11, 2026, the brands took very different creative routes: SYRN turned to a Dr. Seuss character, while SKIMS leaned on family nostalgia.

SYRN's Grinch-inspired collection

Hello! reported that Sweeney appeared in SYRN's holiday collection as Cindy-Lou Who from The Grinch. The campaign featured a flannel set in a colorway called Snow Problem. Hello! added that the styling included white stockings, a baby blue cape trimmed with white fur, and a Cindy-Lou Who-style wig topped with a flannel bow.

The outlet also revisited Sweeney's earlier comments about why she founded the brand. In a press release quoted by Hello!, she said that finding a bra that fit properly as a teenager shaped her thinking, and that designing for different body types remained central to SYRN. She said she wanted the brand to be a place where women could move between the different sides of who they are.

SKIMS brings the whole family

For its own holiday push, SKIMS enlisted Kim Kardashian's sisters Kourtney Kardashian and Khloé Kardashian, along with Kendall Jenner and Kylie Jenner. Hello! said the photographer was Nadia Lee Cohen, and the shoot was set in a vintage-inspired living room with a Christmas tree.

Kim Kardashian spoke about the project to ELLE, as quoted by Hello!. She said it was rare for everyone to get together given their busy schedules, so the campaign meant a lot to the family. She added that the holidays had always been an important time for them.

Two brands, two approaches

Hello! framed the launches as a contrast: a character-driven concept from SYRN against a cozy, family-centered shoot from SKIMS. The outlet did not publish sales figures or any comparison of performance, and neither brand was reported to have commented on the other's campaign.

The timing reflects how competitive the holiday season is for intimates labels, which commonly time collections and marketing pushes to the gift-buying period.

What's next

Both collections are now part of the brands' holiday lineups, and it remains to be seen how they perform commercially. Neither company had released sales data at the time of the Hello! report. For now, the verdict rests with fans, and the debate over which campaign is better is likely to continue on social media through December.

Sydney Sweeney's Next Comedy: What's Actually Confirmed

Apple is developing a remake of the 1964 comedy That Man From Rio with Sydney Sweeney and director Justin Lin. Here is what has been confirmed.

By Sweeney Report · November 18, 2026

Sydney Sweeney is set to lead a remake of the 1964 adventure comedy That Man From Rio, which Apple is developing with Justin Lin directing, according to Deadline. Chase Palmer wrote the script, and Sweeney is also attached as an executive producer. No release date has been reported, and the film was still described as in development when the news first broke.

What was reported

Deadline reported the project in October 2025, and other entertainment outlets, including JoBlo and ComingSoon, repeated the main details. Apple Original Films is behind the remake, and Lin is attached as director. He is best known for several entries in the Fast & Furious franchise. Reports said Lin would produce through his company, Perfect Storm Entertainment. Kevin Walsh would produce through The Walsh Company, where he holds a first-look deal with Apple.

The 1964 original was a French-Italian adventure comedy starring Jean-Paul Belmondo. Coverage of the remake described the older film as a lighthearted spoof of spy adventures, and one outlet said it partly inspired later adventure movies such as Indiana Jones.

A familiar partnership

This is not Sweeney's first project with Apple. She previously starred in the thriller Echo Valley for Apple, which Walsh also produced. Reports about the remake presented the new film as a continuation of that working relationship. As an executive producer, she would also have a say behind the camera as well as in front of it.

What has not been confirmed

When the project was announced, the male lead had not been cast. A search of recent coverage found no newer casting announcement, no start of production and no release date. Apple had also not issued a detailed public statement on the film beyond the original trade reports.

Some fans have wondered whether the film will resemble a broad, absurdist comedy such as Barb and Star Go to Vista Del Mar. Nothing in the reporting says so. What has been reported points to an adventure comedy in the spirit of the original, directed by a filmmaker known for large-scale action.

What's next: The most important open question is who will play the male lead opposite Sweeney. Any casting news, a production schedule or a release window would most likely come from Apple or the trades. Until then, the confirmed facts are the director, the writer, Sweeney's two roles and the studio.

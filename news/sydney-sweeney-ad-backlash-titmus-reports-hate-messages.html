Sydney Sweeney Ad Backlash: Titmus Reveals Thousands of Hate Messages

Olympic great Ariarne Titmus says thousands of hateful messages followed her criticism of Sydney Sweeney's Novig ad, the Sydney Morning Herald reported.

By Sweeney Report · October 28, 2026

Olympic swimming great Ariarne Titmus says she received thousands of hateful messages after publicly criticizing a Sydney Sweeney advertisement, The Sydney Morning Herald reported on October 28, 2026. According to the paper, Titmus was personally attacked over her appearance after speaking up for women's sport. The ad was made for the US sports prediction platform Novig.

The background

The dispute began last month, when Titmus posted a response to the Novig campaign on social media. The Herald reported that the ad played on the company's "just sports" slogan and that Titmus was one of several prominent female athletes to condemn it.

News.com.au and The Cairns Post also covered her original reaction. They reported that Titmus called the ad "an absolute joke" and "the furthest thing from sport." In her post, she said she could not describe how infuriated she felt watching it. She wrote that it was a kick in the face to every woman who has dedicated her life to perfecting her craft, and to every woman who enjoys sport for what it really is.

Support and fallout

The Herald reported that fellow Olympians and other well-known female athletes backed Titmus. Many of them shared criticisms of their own about the advertisement.

The reaction to her post also drew attention online. SSBCrack published a report headlined around the backlash Titmus faced for her comments. The Herald's account of the volume of messages comes from Titmus herself. The material reviewed did not include an independent count.

Why it matters

The episode shows how a single brand campaign can become a wider argument about how women are represented in and around sport. Titmus framed her objection around respect for athletes. Her critics, according to the Herald's reporting, responded with personal attacks rather than engaging with that point. Opinions on the ad itself are divided, and each view belongs to the person who voiced it.

Sweeney is the face of the campaign, but the reports reviewed attribute the criticism to Titmus and other athletes. They did not quote Sweeney or Novig in response to the latest remarks.

What's next

It is not yet clear whether Sweeney or Novig will respond to Titmus's comments or to the wider reaction. The Sweeney Report will update this story if either side speaks publicly.

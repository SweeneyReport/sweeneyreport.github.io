Sydney Sweeney Backed as Bond Girl, But She Wants a Different Role

Sydney Sweeney has drawn support for a Bond girl role opposite Robert Pattinson, but she told Variety she would have more fun playing James Bond.

By Sweeney Report · November 21, 2026

Sydney Sweeney has once again been backed by fans and industry figures as a possible Bond girl opposite Robert Pattinson, the new 007. Yardbarker reported that she has a different idea, though. Sweeney told Variety that casting would depend on the script, adding that she would have more fun as James Bond herself.

Background: A new 007 and a casting guessing game

Yardbarker reported that the franchise recently announced Pattinson as the next Bond, replacing Daniel Craig. Social media largely celebrated the choice, and attention quickly moved to who might play the female lead. Names such as Zendaya and Margot Robbie have circulated in the rumor mill, according to Yardbarker, and The Week also listed Sweeney and Zendaya among the leading names in the buzz. Amazon MGM has not publicly named anyone, and its position on the role was described as unknown.

Who has backed Sweeney

Comedian and commentator Alex Stein said, "If it's not Sydney Sweeney, then it's not a James Bond movie." News24 Australia reported his comments, per Yardbarker.

Trina Parks, who played Thumper in the 1971 Bond film Diamonds Are Forever, had also suggested Sweeney in a conversation with The Telegraph. Parks pointed to the range of work Sweeney has done and said she had ownership of her own identity as an actress.

Director Paul Feig, who worked with Sweeney on The Housemaid, told The Sun that he believed she could take on a Bond project. Yardbarker noted that he had voiced that view earlier.

Sweeney's own take

Despite the enthusiasm for a Bond girl part, Sweeney appeared to have a different preference. Yardbarker reported that she suggested she could make a better spy than a love interest. Her comment to Variety left room for negotiation, as she tied any decision to the quality of the script, while joking that playing the lead agent would be more fun.

Pattinson is replacing Craig as 007, and a snippet of him in the role has reportedly already generated excitement among fans, according to Yardbarker.

What's next: No Bond girl has been announced, and Amazon MGM has not confirmed any casting talk involving Sweeney. Until an official announcement arrives, speculation is likely to continue, and any role for Sweeney remains unconfirmed.

Sydney Sweeney Worked With Amy Adams Before Euphoria

Before Euphoria, Sydney Sweeney appeared opposite Amy Adams in HBO's 2018 miniseries Sharp Objects, playing the recurring role of Alice.

By Sweeney Report · November 16, 2026

Sydney Sweeney was already working with an Oscar-nominated lead on HBO before she became known for Euphoria. In 2018 she played Alice in Sharp Objects, an eight-episode miniseries starring Amy Adams. It premiered on July 8, 2018, and Euphoria followed on the same network a year later.

The role in Sharp Objects

The series was adapted from Gillian Flynn's 2006 debut novel. Adams played Camille Preaker, a reporter who goes back to her hometown of Wind Gap, Missouri, to cover the murders of two young girls. Patricia Clarkson played Camille's mother, Adora. Sweeney's Alice was a recurring character, a roommate Camille met at a psychiatric facility.

Jean-Marc Vallée directed all eight episodes, and Marti Noxon created the series. According to Wikipedia's account of Sweeney's career, the part grew beyond its original size because the director kept bringing the character back. The same source says she prepared by reading about girls dealing with mental illness and self-harm, and by visiting hospitals.

How the series was received

The show did well with awards voters. Clarkson won the Golden Globe for best supporting actress in a limited series or television film, and Adams was nominated in the lead category. At the 71st Primetime Emmy Awards, Sharp Objects received eight nominations, including outstanding limited series. Both Adams and Clarkson were among the acting nominees. Wikipedia reports that the premiere drew about 1.54 million live viewers in the U.S. and the finale about 1.76 million.

A busy 2018

Sharp Objects was one of several television jobs Sweeney held that year. She starred as Emaline Addario in Netflix's Everything Sucks!, set among Oregon high school students in 1996. She also had a recurring part as Eden Spencer in the second season of The Handmaid's Tale. In June 2019, she was cast as Cassie Howard in Euphoria, the role that brought her wide attention. It later earned her an Emmy nomination for supporting actress in a drama series at the 74th Primetime Emmy Awards in 2022.

Why it matters

Looking back, Sharp Objects places Sweeney in a prestige HBO production alongside experienced co-stars before her best-known part. Her work that year across HBO and Netflix shows how many different projects she was involved in before Euphoria.

Sydney Sweeney: Only One Upcoming Film Has a Release Date

Only The Housemaid's Secret has a release date so far, while Gundam and The Custom of the Country are still waiting for theirs.

By Sweeney Report · November 18, 2026

Of Sydney Sweeney's next big projects, only one has a firm release date. Variety reported that The Housemaid's Secret, the sequel to her hit thriller The Housemaid, is set to open in theaters on December 17, 2027. Kirsten Dunst has reportedly joined the cast. Two other Sweeney films, Netflix's live-action Gundam and the period drama The Custom of the Country, have both started or finished production but have no announced release dates.

The Housemaid sequel

Lionsgate announced the December 2027 date, and trade outlets including Variety and Screen Daily covered the news. Sweeney returns for the follow-up, and reports said Michele Morrone is back as well. Dunst is new to the franchise, and outlets have given conflicting accounts of her character's name, so that detail remains unclear.

The original film performed strongly at the box office, with coverage describing a worldwide total of close to $400 million. The sequel's date puts it two years after the first film. According to Variety's reporting, it will also open against Avengers: Secret Wars and The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum, which are both scheduled for the same weekend.

Gundam is done shooting, with no date

Netflix's live-action Gundam finished principal photography in July, according to several outlets. Noah Centineo is among the cast, and Sweeney stars in the film. Reports said the production was shot in Queensland, Australia, under the working title Teardrop, and that Jim Mickle is directing for Legendary Pictures with Bandai Namco Filmworks. Netflix had not announced a release date at the time of writing, although coverage generally expected a 2027 arrival.

The Custom of the Country

Josie Rourke is directing this adaptation of Edith Wharton's novel, and she also adapted the script. Sweeney plays Undine Spragg, and Leo Woodall co-stars. Screen Global Production reported that StudioCanal and its partners announced cameras rolling in Portugal in March 2026, and filming began in February. No release date has been announced.

What's next

For now, December 17, 2027 is the only date fans can mark in the calendar. Netflix has yet to schedule Gundam, and The Custom of the Country is still in the pipeline, so more release news could arrive in the coming months.

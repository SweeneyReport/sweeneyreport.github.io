Sydney Sweeney Could Soon Be Meghan Markle's Neighbor

Reports say Sydney Sweeney is looking at Cotswolds properties, and a News24 panel joked about her possible famous neighbors, Harry and Meghan among them.

By Sweeney Report · November 7, 2026

Sydney Sweeney could reportedly soon share a neighborhood with Meghan Markle. Reality Tea reported that the Euphoria star was considering properties in the Cotswolds, an area of the English countryside that Prince Harry and Meghan also reportedly visit. The talk drew attention after a segment on News24, hosted by Rita Panahi. Neither Sweeney nor the Sussexes' representatives were quoted as confirming any move.

What was said on News24

According to Reality Tea, Panahi brought up the reports while moving on to Sweeney, saying the actress was reportedly planning a move to England. She joked that Sweeney might end up with some very famous neighbors, naming Harry and Meghan as well as Ellen and Portia.

A guest on the program then took a swipe at Meghan. The guest suggested that Harry and Meghan might finally get a Hollywood neighbor who could explain how to build a successful entertainment career. The same guest said that with Ellen, the Beckhams and possibly Sweeney in the area, the Cotswolds was turning into Beverly Hills with better architecture and worse weather. The guest added that they understood why the region appealed to wealthy and famous people. These were the guest's opinions, as relayed by Reality Tea.

The career comparison behind the joke

Reality Tea noted that Meghan's career has taken several turns since she left Suits in 2017. After Harry and Meghan stepped back from the royal family in 2020, they signed a Netflix deal through their company, Archewell Productions. Their documentary series Harry & Meghan was reportedly a major streaming success, while other projects, including Live to Lead and Polo, drew less attention.

Her lifestyle series With Love, Meghan also received a mixed response, the outlet wrote. Its first season reportedly drew 5.3 million views in the first half of 2025, while the second season reportedly recorded two million views between July and December of that year.

Sweeney, by contrast, has built a prominent acting career, Reality Tea said, with credits that include Euphoria, The White Lotus and Anyone But You.

What's next

For now, the story rests on reports of Sweeney looking at homes, and nothing has been confirmed about a purchase or a move. Whether she actually settles in the Cotswolds remains to be seen. Any announcement would most likely come from Sweeney or her team.

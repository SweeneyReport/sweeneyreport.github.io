Sydney Sweeney's Housemaid Is Back on Top of HBO Max

Sydney Sweeney's R-rated thriller The Housemaid reached the top of HBO Max after its October 9 arrival, with a sequel already greenlit by Lionsgate.

By Sweeney Report · October 24, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's thriller The Housemaid climbed back to number one on HBO Max. The R-rated film, which also stars Amanda Seyfried, arrived on the streaming service on October 9. According to the tracking site FlixPatrol, it held the top spot in the United States on October 11. The movie earned about $400 million worldwide in theaters, on a budget of roughly $35 million, and Lionsgate has already greenlit a sequel.

From theaters to streaming

The Housemaid performed strongly in cinemas before reaching HBO Max. Its worldwide gross of about $400 million, set against a production budget of roughly $35 million, made it a notable financial success for Lionsgate, the studio behind the film.

That success carried over to streaming. IMDb described the thriller in a headline as one of the biggest titles of 2026 on streaming so far. ScreenRant, meanwhile, placed it first on its list of three movies to watch on HBO Max over a weekend, and called it a box office hit.

Why it matters for Sweeney

The result gives Sweeney another commercial win in a lead role. The film pairs her with Seyfried in a story built around suspense, and its performance on both the big screen and a streaming platform suggests audiences were still seeking it out after its theatrical run. FlixPatrol's ranking reflects viewing activity on the day it was recorded, so the number one position was a snapshot and not a lasting measure of the film's reach.

Neither IMDb nor ScreenRant was cited here for the exact box office figures. The gross and budget numbers are approximate, as reported around the film's release.

The sequel

Lionsgate has greenlit a follow-up to The Housemaid. This report does not include details on its cast, story or release date, and none had been confirmed in the material reviewed.

What's next: Viewers can keep watching The Housemaid on HBO Max while Lionsgate develops the sequel. Any news about casting, a start of production or a release schedule will be covered here as it is announced.

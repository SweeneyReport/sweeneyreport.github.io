Sydney Sweeney's Christy: Training, Weight Gain and Box Office

Sydney Sweeney trained for months and reportedly gained about 30 pounds to play boxer Christy Martin, but the biopic opened to roughly $1.3 million.

By Sweeney Report · November 14, 2026

Sydney Sweeney spent months preparing to play boxing champion Christy Martin in Christy, a biopic directed by David Michôd. The film opened in U.S. theaters on November 7, 2025, and took in about $1.3 million on its opening weekend, according to box office reports. The Numbers lists a production budget of $15 million, though other outlets said the budget was never officially disclosed.

The training and the transformation

Sweeney told W Magazine that she gained about 30 pounds for the part and went up four pant sizes. Far Out Magazine and NBC News both covered her comments about the preparation. Reports described a routine that combined weightlifting and kickboxing over roughly three and a half months. According to those accounts, she trained in the morning, did a longer kickboxing session midday and lifted again at night. E! reported that her diet included a lot of fast food as part of the weight gain.

Martin was reportedly closely involved in the production and offered guidance and feedback. At the Hamptons International Film Festival, Sweeney said she did her own boxing scenes without a stunt double. She said the fights on screen were real and that the actors were actually hitting each other. The cast also includes Ben Foster, Merritt Wever and Katy M. O'Brian.

A tough box office debut

The Wrap reported that Christy ranked among the weakest wide-release openings on record. Complex reported an average of $649 per theater. Box Office Mojo's figures indicate the film played in about 2,000 theaters. GamesRadar noted that the film's Rotten Tomatoes score was decent, at about 67 percent when it wrote about the opening, so the weak turnout did not match the reviews. The reports also pointed out that it was Sweeney's third 2025 release to earn under $2 million on its opening weekend, after Eden and Americana.

Hypebeast and Dark Horizons both covered Sweeney's public response to the box office result. The sources differ on some totals: CNN reportedly put the film's later cumulative gross higher than the opening-weekend figure, which is normal as ticket sales continue to add up.

Where to watch

After its theatrical run, the film moved to digital platforms in December 2025, giving audiences another way to see the performance that drew most of the pre-release attention.

What's next

The box office numbers have not stopped coverage of Sweeney's work in the ring. Whether she returns to a sports drama remains to be seen. For now, Christy stands as a physically demanding role that critics reviewed more favorably than audiences supported it in theaters.

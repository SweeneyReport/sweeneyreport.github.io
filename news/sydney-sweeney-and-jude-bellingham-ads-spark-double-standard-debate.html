Sydney Sweeney vs Jude Bellingham: The Ad Double Standard Debate

Commentator Kinsey Schofield said reactions to Bellingham's Calvin Klein ad differ from the backlash Sydney Sweeney faced over her American Eagle campaign.

By Sweeney Report · November 7, 2026

Jude Bellingham's new Calvin Klein campaign has revived the debate over how advertisements are judged, with commentator Kinsey Schofield arguing that reactions to his ad differ sharply from those that followed Sydney Sweeney's American Eagle campaign. Reality Tea reported that Schofield made the comments in a conversation with host Rita Panahi, who pushed back on some of the online responses to the footballer.

The background

According to Reality Tea, Sweeney faced intense backlash in 2025 over her American Eagle ad. Some critics said the campaign sexualized her, while others took issue with its tagline, "Sydney Sweeney has great jeans," a play on the phrase "great genes." Reality Tea reported that some people argued the wording promoted a racial ideal during a politically charged period.

Bellingham, an English professional footballer, is fronting Calvin Klein's Icon Cotton Stretch underwear campaign, his first for the brand. Reality Tea reported that the campaign drew heavy social media attention and has reportedly lifted the company's stock by 6%.

The reactions

Reality Tea reported that Schofield compared the two responses, saying Sweeney is pulled into a national conversation about the male gaze whenever she appears in a campaign, while Bellingham's ad was met with enthusiasm. She asked where the lectures about objectification were this time.

After being shown some of the reactions from women, Panahi said, according to Reality Tea, that some of them should hold themselves to a higher standard. She described Bellingham as a human being with feelings, not someone to be ogled, and said she found that kind of objectification offensive. These are the opinions of Schofield and Panahi as reported by the outlet.

The Novig ad

Reality Tea also noted that Sweeney drew a fresh round of criticism over her ad for the sports trading app Novig. The outlet reported that, while she has previously addressed the American Eagle controversy, she has not commented on the Novig ad.

What's next: Neither Sweeney nor Bellingham was reported to have responded to the double standard debate. Whether Sweeney will address the Novig criticism remains unclear, and the conversation over how celebrity ads are judged shows no sign of fading.

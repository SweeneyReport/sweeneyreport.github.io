Sydney Sweeney's SYRN Reportedly Hits Record Sales Despite Backlash

Page Six reported that Sydney Sweeney's SYRN brand doubled its third-quarter sales over the second and is on track for another record month in October.

By Sweeney Report · October 29, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's SYRN brand reportedly had the strongest quarter since its launch. Page Six reported on October 28, 2026, that the label doubled its third-quarter sales compared with the second quarter. The outlet cited an unnamed source and described the results as record-breaking. Several products also reportedly sold out. The claims have not been independently confirmed.

The numbers behind the report

According to Page Six, the first source said SYRN's third-quarter sales were double those of the previous quarter. That source also said the brand generated more than three times its June sales. A second source close to the brand told the outlet that September was the company's biggest sales month. That source reportedly added that sales would have been higher still if some products had not sold out early.

Page Six also reported that a birthday-themed collection produced the brand's biggest single sales day to date. The outlet said the Blue Ditsy set sold out on its first day. The launch reportedly brought in more than $2 million in sales, and the collection sold out within a single day, according to the source.

Momentum into the holidays

Page Six said October was on track for a further 20 percent increase over September. If that held, it would reportedly mark another monthly sales record. The outlet linked the growth to Sweeney's ongoing promotional campaigns, including a holiday collection. As Page Six previously reported, the holiday campaign drew on Christmas classics such as Home Alone and How the Grinch Stole Christmas.

Page Six's headline said the growth came "despite haters." The article did not detail specific criticism of the brand or its advertising. It framed the sales figures as a sign of strong consumer demand for the label.

A brand building its own identity

The report described SYRN as increasingly positioning itself as a standalone fashion business rather than only a celebrity-founded label. Page Six said the company was drawing on Sweeney's cultural influence, direct engagement with customers and demand for playful, accessible designs. According to the outlet, the brand's next phase focused on expanding its product range and improving inventory availability, so that fewer items would sell out so quickly.

A representative for Sweeney did not immediately respond to Page Six's request for comment. SYRN has not publicly confirmed the figures, which came entirely from unnamed sources.

What's next

The next test will be whether October's reported 20 percent gain materialized, and how the brand handled inventory through the holiday season. Official figures or a statement from Sweeney's team would confirm whether these reported records hold up. Sweeney Report will follow any updates.

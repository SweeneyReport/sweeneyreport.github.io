Sydney Sweeney in the Race for Pattinson's Bond Girl?

Reports name Sydney Sweeney among possible leads opposite Robert Pattinson in the next James Bond, but nothing has been confirmed or announced.

By Sweeney Report · November 18, 2026

Sydney Sweeney was named among the actors reportedly in the running to play the next Bond girl opposite Robert Pattinson. NDTV Profit reported that Margaret Qualley led early bets, with Sweeney and Zendaya reportedly following behind. CinéSérie published a similar list that also included Florence Pugh. None of this is confirmed, and no casting has been officially announced.

What the reports said

NDTV Profit framed its piece as a look at early bets on who might star alongside Pattinson in the upcoming James Bond film. In that outlet's ordering, Qualley was the front-runner, while Sweeney and Zendaya were described as chasing her.

CinéSérie, reporting via MSN, took a comparable approach. It asked who could become the next Bond girl and listed Sweeney, Qualley, Zendaya and Pugh as possibilities. Both outlets presented the topic as a speculative race rather than a confirmed shortlist.

Speculation, not casting news

It is important to separate the lists from the facts. Neither outlet reported an official announcement from the studio or the production, and the names appear to reflect betting-style guesses and fan-style speculation. Nothing in the source material indicated that any of the actresses had been approached, had auditioned or had entered talks.

Sweeney's name appearing on such lists is, for now, a sign of how often she is mentioned in casting conversations rather than evidence of a real negotiation. The same applies to the other names. Qualley being listed first by NDTV Profit reflects that outlet's view of the early odds, not an insider confirmation.

Why the lists matter to fans

Any casting around a new James Bond draws heavy attention, and pairing the franchise with Pattinson has fueled interest in who might appear opposite him. Lists like these tend to circulate quickly online, which is why it is worth treating them carefully until an official source speaks.

What's next

For now, readers should wait for an official statement on the film's cast. Until the production confirms anything, Sweeney, Qualley, Zendaya and Pugh remain names on speculative lists only. The Sweeney Report will update this story if a casting announcement is made.

Sydney Sweeney Is Filming The Housemaid Sequel in New Jersey

Set photos published October 28 show Sydney Sweeney shooting The Housemaid's Secret, the sequel to the 2025 hit that earned over $400 million worldwide.

By Sweeney Report · November 1, 2026

Sydney Sweeney was photographed filming The Housemaid's Secret on city streets in New Jersey, according to images published on October 28 by The U.S. Sun. The movie is the sequel to The Housemaid, the 2025 thriller that became one of the year's notable box office successes. Men's Journal, in a piece syndicated by AOL, also reported on the set photos the following day.

What the set photos show

The U.S. Sun reported that Sweeney was seen shooting scenes outdoors in New Jersey. Men's Journal said the pictures were circulating widely online and described them as a first look at production on the follow-up. Neither outlet, as quoted in the coverage, revealed plot details, and the studio had not reportedly released any official comment on the images. Several outlets framed the photos as a sign that the sequel would keep the suspenseful tone of the first film.

The original film's success

According to Men's Journal, The Housemaid earned more than $400 million worldwide on an estimated budget of $35 million. The outlet also reported that the film later became a major streaming hit. The thriller follows a woman with a troubled past who takes a live-in housekeeping job for a wealthy couple, and the situation grows increasingly tense.

Sweeney starred alongside Amanda Seyfried and Brandon Sklenar. Men's Journal reported that the film's strong performance led to the greenlight for a sequel, which carries the title The Housemaid's Secret.

Why it matters

The sequel is an important project for Sweeney, who has built a varied slate that spans series, thrillers, comedies and producing work. A follow-up to a film that multiplied its budget many times over in ticket sales will face high expectations, both from fans of the original and from the industry watching how the franchise develops. Sequels to surprise hits often find it hard to match the first installment's momentum, so the comparison with the original's numbers is likely to follow the new film through its release.

What's next

No release date was mentioned in the coverage reviewed, and it was not confirmed in those reports whether Seyfried and Sklenar are returning. Production appeared to be underway in New Jersey as of late October. Further details on the cast, the plot and the schedule are expected to emerge as filming continues.

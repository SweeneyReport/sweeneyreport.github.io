Sydney Sweeney's SYRN Sales Reportedly Doubled in One Quarter

Barstool Sports reports that Sydney Sweeney's lingerie brand SYRN doubled its sales from Q2 to Q3 2026, helped by a birthday collection that sold out in a day.

By Sweeney Report · November 21, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's lingerie brand SYRN reportedly doubled its sales in a single quarter. Barstool Sports reported on November 20, 2026 that third-quarter sales were twice as high as in the second quarter. The outlet also said they were more than triple the figures for June. The numbers come from Barstool's report and have not been confirmed by Sweeney or the company.

The numbers behind the growth

According to Barstool Sports, the main driver was SYRN's birthday collection. The outlet reported that the release produced the brand's biggest sales day to date, with more than $2 million in revenue, and that it sold out within 24 hours.

Barstool also reported that September set a monthly sales record for the brand. It added that October was projected to grow by roughly another 20% compared with September. That figure is a projection, not a confirmed result, and no breakdown of the underlying sales data was provided in the report.

A new winter photoshoot

The sales report came in a post about a new winter-themed photoshoot. Barstool Sports said Sweeney worked on it with model Brooklin Laird. The outlet also noted that the models SYRN hires have been part of the brand's appeal. Barstool did not say where the photos would be used or whether they were tied to an upcoming SYRN release.

Why it matters for her career

SYRN has become one of Sweeney's most visible ventures outside acting, alongside her film and television work. If the reported figures are accurate, they suggest the brand is gaining commercial momentum quickly, and that its product drops can generate heavy demand in a short time. A sell-out within a day also shows how much attention a single launch can draw.

Barstool's coverage was openly enthusiastic about Sweeney's business success, and the outlet framed the growth as a response to her critics. That framing is Barstool's own view. The sales claims are the part of the report that bears on her business, and they rest on a single outlet's account.

What's next: The next test for SYRN will be whether October's reported growth materializes and whether the brand can keep its momentum going into the holiday season. Neither Sweeney nor SYRN had publicly confirmed the sales figures at the time of writing. Any official statement or additional reporting would show whether this is a lasting trend or a strong run driven by one popular launch.

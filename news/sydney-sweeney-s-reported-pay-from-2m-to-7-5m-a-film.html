Sydney Sweeney's Reported Pay: $2M to $7.5M

TMZ reported Sydney Sweeney would earn $7.5 million for The Housemaid, up from a reported $2 million for Anyone But You. All figures are unconfirmed.

By Sweeney Report · November 20, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's reported pay rose sharply in the space of one film. TMZ reported on October 31, 2024 that she would earn $7.5 million to star in The Housemaid, a Lionsgate thriller. The same report said she was paid about $2 million for Anyone But You. None of these numbers has been confirmed by the actress or the studios, so they should be read as press estimates.

From Anyone But You to The Housemaid

TMZ's article said Sweeney earned $2 million for the Sony romantic comedy Anyone But You, plus a further $250,000 as an executive producer. The outlet presented the film's commercial success as the reason her next deal was so much larger. It also compared her earnings with those of other actors of her generation. For instance, it mentioned Zendaya's reported $10 million for Challengers.

According to TMZ, the $7.5 million figure was the biggest deal of her career to that point. TMZ's coverage did not give a figure for her work on Euphoria. Pay estimates for that series circulate widely online and vary a lot. None of them is official, and this article does not treat any of them as fact.

The film behind the reported deal

The Housemaid was directed by Paul Feig and distributed by Lionsgate. Sweeney plays Millie Calloway, and Amanda Seyfried plays Nina Winchester. Brandon Sklenar and Michele Morrone also star. The film premiered in New York on December 2, 2025 and opened in US theaters on December 19, 2025. According to Wikipedia, which compiles box office data, it earned about $400.5 million worldwide against a reported budget of $35 million.

Why the numbers are uncertain

Hollywood does not publish actors' contracts. Salary figures usually come from anonymous sources, and they can leave out bonuses, backend participation or producer fees. Several online "net worth" pages also repeat the same numbers without checking them, which makes them look more solid than they are. The $2 million and $7.5 million figures come from TMZ's reporting and should be attributed to that outlet.

What's next

Neither Sweeney nor Lionsgate has commented publicly on the reported figures. Her pay will probably keep being tracked as her projects continue to perform. Should actors' salaries be public? Tell us what you think.

Sydney Sweeney's R-Rated Rom-Com Is Back on HBO Max Charts

Three years after release, Anyone But You rose from ninth to sixth on HBO Max's U.S. chart, just below Sydney Sweeney's The Housemaid, FlixPatrol data shows.

By Sweeney Report · November 2, 2026

Anyone But You, the 2023 R-rated romantic comedy starring Sydney Sweeney and Glen Powell, is climbing the HBO Max charts again. Comic Book Resources reported, citing FlixPatrol, that the film entered the U.S. chart in ninth place on October 27 and had moved up to sixth by November 1. Sweeney's newer release, The Housemaid, sat higher in second place after 21 days on the platform.

A second life on streaming

According to CBR, the renewed interest comes in a busy year for the actress. The outlet described 2026 as a successful one for her, with Euphoria Season 3 marking her farewell to Cassie Howard and The Housemaid starting a new franchise. Men's Journal also covered the chart rise, describing the comedy as one of HBO Max's top movies.

The result means two Sweeney films were on the same U.S. chart at once, according to the FlixPatrol figures CBR cited.

The film's background

Anyone But You was directed by Will Gluck, who co-wrote the script with Ilana Wolpert. It loosely adapts William Shakespeare's Much Ado About Nothing. Sweeney and Powell play Bea and Ben, two people whose first date goes badly. Six months later they end up at the same destination wedding, because Bea's sister is dating Ben's friend's sister. To avoid disrupting the celebration, they agree to pretend to be a couple.

CBR noted that the film was a commercial success. It grossed $220 million worldwide against a $25 million budget, despite mixed reviews. The outlet said the film helped establish Sweeney as a romantic lead in 2023.

What the numbers show

Streaming charts like FlixPatrol's track daily rankings on each platform. They do not give total viewing figures, so the data does not show how many people watched. The positions do show that the film kept drawing attention long after its theatrical run.

What's next: It is not yet known how long Anyone But You will stay on the HBO Max chart. The Housemaid is still in second place, so the next few days of rankings will show whether either film moves. Neither Sweeney nor HBO Max had commented on the chart movement in the coverage reviewed.

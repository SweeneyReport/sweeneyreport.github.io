Sydney Sweeney's Housemaid Hits a Huge Streaming Milestone

The R-rated thriller passed its first 30 days on HBO Max, ScreenRant reported, while a sequel is set for December 17, 2027.

By Sweeney Report · November 13, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's thriller The Housemaid has reached a notable streaming landmark. ScreenRant reported on November 10, 2026, that the R-rated psychological thriller has completed its first 30 days on HBO Max, a milestone the outlet framed as part of a broader run of records for the film. IMDb also described it as one of the biggest streaming hits in the world. A sequel, The Housemaid's Secret, is already on the calendar.

A career-best performer

According to ScreenRant, The Housemaid is the highest-grossing film of Sweeney's career so far, with roughly $400.5 million earned worldwide at the box office. That figure places it well ahead of her earlier big-screen work, which includes the romantic comedy Anyone but You with Glen Powell and a role in Quentin Tarantino's Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, both cited by the outlet.

The strong theatrical run now appears to be carrying over to streaming. ScreenRant said the film is chasing further streaming records, and IMDb's coverage likewise highlighted its standing among the most-watched titles worldwide on the platform.

The sequel and her slate

ScreenRant reported that The Housemaid's Secret will continue the story as another R-rated psychological thriller, with Sweeney returning as Millie Calloway. The sequel is scheduled to open in theaters on December 17, 2027.

The outlet also noted that the sequel is one of several projects on her plate. It listed a live-action adaptation of the video game Split Fiction, the sci-fi film Gundam opposite Noah Centineo on Netflix, and The Custom of the Country with Leo Woodall, among others.

Why it matters

A hit that performs both in theaters and on streaming strengthens Sweeney's position as a lead actor who can carry a film commercially. It also gives the studio a strong argument for expanding the franchise. Whether the follow-up can match the original's numbers remains an open question, and no box office projections for it were included in the reports reviewed.

What's next: Attention now turns to The Housemaid's Secret, due December 17, 2027, and to whether the original keeps climbing the streaming charts in the coming weeks. Sweeney's other announced projects are expected to follow in the meantime.

Sydney Sweeney Recreates Home Alone Scream In New SYRN Ad

Sydney Sweeney played Kevin McCallister in a new Instagram ad for her brand Syrn, which promotes its Hibernation Nation holiday collection.

By Sweeney Report · November 1, 2026

Sydney Sweeney recreated one of the best-known moments from Home Alone in a new Instagram ad for Syrn, her lingerie and loungewear brand. USA Today reported that the Euphoria star, 29, took on the role of Macaulay Culkin's Kevin McCallister and revisited the aftershave scene from the 1990 Christmas comedy. The spot promotes Syrn's Hibernation Nation holiday collection.

What happens in the ad

According to USA Today, Sweeney copies Kevin's grooming routine from the film and ends with the character's open-mouthed scream, her hands pressed to her face. TheRichest reported that other parts of the campaign show her holding a small dog and eating a large ice cream sundae.

The ad is part of Syrn's holiday line. TheRichest listed the Hibernation Nation range as including lounge pants, cardigans, shorts, cropped tanks and cable-knit sweaters. It also includes sweaters made for dogs.

The film behind the joke

The scene comes from a major box office hit. TheRichest reported that Home Alone earned $476.7 million worldwide. It was the highest-grossing live-action comedy until The Hangover Part II overtook it in 2011. Using a holiday classic fits the seasonal timing of the collection.

Coming after the Novig backlash

USA Today noted that the Syrn ad follows criticism of Sweeney's campaign for Novig, a sports betting platform. TheRichest reported that she shared a second Novig video in which the ad criticized rival platforms over questionable betting markets. Neither outlet suggested the Syrn spot was a response to that criticism. The two campaigns do show how much of her public profile now comes from brand partnerships and her own business.

Syrn did not appear to have released further details about the collection beyond what the outlets described. Sales figures for the line had not been reported.

What's next

Syrn's holiday push is still underway, and more Hibernation Nation content may follow as the season goes on. Whether Sweeney does another movie-inspired ad has not been announced.

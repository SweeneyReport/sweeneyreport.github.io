Sydney Sweeney's Reported Move Has Cotswolds Prices in Focus

The Daily Mail reported that Sydney Sweeney is looking at homes in Little Tew, as celebrity arrivals fuel debate over rising Cotswolds prices.

By Sweeney Report · November 8, 2026

Sydney Sweeney is reportedly house-hunting in the Cotswolds, according to the Daily Mail, which said the actress has been looking at properties in Little Tew, a tiny village with no shops and no pubs. The report landed amid a wider discussion about how a wave of famous arrivals may be affecting what visitors pay in the English countryside region.

The background

The Daily Mail reported that Sweeney spent last Christmas in the Cotswolds. The outlet described the region as a growing magnet for Hollywood names, listing Ellen DeGeneres and Portia de Rossi, who reportedly live there permanently, along with Beyoncé, Jay-Z and JD Vance as visitors. It also said Taylor Swift based herself nearby during her 2024 tour.

The paper added that Prince Harry and Meghan Markle moved to the area in late August after years in Montecito, California, and received the keys to a permanent home the previous weekend.

Prices in focus

The Daily Mail argued that long-time holidaymakers have watched costs climb. By its figures, a night in December at The Bell in Charlbury, a pub it said Queen Camilla is known to like, cost £477. The paper said that adding lunch pushed a weekend for two above £1,000.

It also reported that cabins at Soho Farmhouse, the members-only retreat, cost up to £1,200 a night. In addition, it said the cabin brand CABÜ planned to open a £24 million resort next year, a short drive from that property.

The debate

The article suggested that the news about Sweeney would likely draw eye-rolls from locals and from domestic tourists who have noticed sharper prices. That is the Daily Mail's framing, and the piece did not attribute the price rises to any single celebrity. Sweeney has not publicly confirmed a move, and the house-hunting claim remains a report.

What's next: Neither Sweeney nor her representatives had confirmed any purchase at the time of writing. Whether the reported resort opening and further celebrity arrivals change the region's pricing is something travellers and locals will be watching closely.

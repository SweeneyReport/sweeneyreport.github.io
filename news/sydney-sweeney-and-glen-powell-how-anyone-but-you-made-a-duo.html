Sydney Sweeney & Glen Powell: How a Rom-Com Made Them a Duo

A modest Christmas opening grew into a roughly $220 million hit, and Sydney Sweeney and Glen Powell now say they want to work together again.

By Sweeney Report · November 15, 2026

Sydney Sweeney and Glen Powell became one of Hollywood's most talked-about on-screen pairs through Anyone But You, a romantic comedy directed by Will Gluck. The film opened modestly in December 2023, then grew into a worldwide hit of roughly $220 million. Both stars have since said they would like to team up again, but no sequel or new joint project has been confirmed.

How the film came together

Anyone But You was a loose adaptation of Shakespeare's Much Ado About Nothing. Gluck co-wrote the screenplay with Ilana Wolpert, and Wikipedia's entry on the film lists a production budget of about $25 million. Filming began in New South Wales, Australia, in early 2023, with scenes shot at locations around Sydney. Sony Pictures Releasing distributed it in North America.

A slow-burn box office run

The film opened in the United States and Canada on December 22, 2023. According to figures compiled on Wikipedia, it earned about $6.3 million over the four-day Christmas weekend and placed fourth, close to the roughly $7 million that had been projected. Rather than fading, it kept drawing audiences through the holidays and into the new year.

Entertainment outlets including /Film and Yahoo Entertainment later reported that it had passed $100 million worldwide. The Numbers listed its worldwide total at about $219 million, and Wikipedia put the figure near $220 million, with about $88 million from the U.S. and Canada and about $132 million from other markets. That made it an unusually large result for a mid-budget rom-com.

Talk of a reunion

Sweeney addressed a possible follow-up in several interviews in early 2024. People reported that she said she and Powell were "dreaming up" ideas for a sequel. TheWrap reported that she told Jimmy Fallon on The Tonight Show there was a high chance of one happening, without giving any story details. Powell was reportedly more playful and less specific when asked about it.

Powell also said in an interview covered by Yahoo that the pair leaned into romance rumors to promote the film, and that the approach worked. Neither actor has described those rumors as fact, and the press has not confirmed any off-screen relationship.

What's next

As of now, neither Sony nor the filmmakers have announced a sequel to Anyone But You or another Sweeney and Powell collaboration. The stars' public enthusiasm and the film's box office results leave the door open, but any reunion remains unconfirmed.

Sydney Sweeney Gets Backed by a Golf Star in Novig Ad Row

English golfer Charley Hull defended Sydney Sweeney over her Novig betting ad, saying modeling is part of the actress's job, CCN reported.

By Sweeney Report · October 29, 2026

English golfer Charley Hull has stepped into the debate over Sydney Sweeney's advertising work for the betting platform Novig. According to CCN, Hull defended the actress and said modeling is quite literally part of Sweeney's job. CCN also reported that one critic of the campaign now says the backlash is outweighing the support.

What Hull said

CCN reported that Hull came to Sweeney's defense after the ad drew criticism. The golfer's central argument, as the outlet described it, was that appearing in campaigns is simply what a professional model and actress does for a living. She reportedly also questioned why some of the people objecting to the ad share bikini photos on their own social media accounts.

The comments added a voice from the world of professional sport to a dispute that had so far centered on Sweeney and the brand behind the campaign.

The critic and the backlash

CCN reported that one critic of the advertisement said they were facing what the outlet described as twice the hate, and that the negative reaction was now outweighing the support they had received. The outlet did not frame this as settled opinion, and it is unclear how widely that view is shared. The specific objections to the ad are laid out in CCN's report, and readers should treat each viewpoint as that of the person who voiced it.

The episode shows how a single advertising campaign can turn into a wider public argument, with athletes, commentators and fans all weighing in on social media.

Novig's position

Novig has said that Sweeney is a partner in the company, according to the CCN report. That description suggests her involvement goes beyond a typical one-off endorsement, though the details of the arrangement were not spelled out in the material available to us. We have not independently confirmed the terms of her deal with the company.

Why it matters

Sweeney has built a high-profile career across film, television and brand partnerships, and her commercial projects regularly draw attention alongside her acting work. Hull's remarks highlight a recurring question in the debate: whether criticism of a campaign is aimed at the work itself, or whether it overlooks the fact that advertising is a normal part of an entertainer's business.

What's next: Neither Sweeney nor Novig was reported to have responded directly to Hull's comments. It remains to be seen whether more public figures join the conversation or whether the campaign continues to be a flashpoint. Sweeney Report will update this story if either side comments further.

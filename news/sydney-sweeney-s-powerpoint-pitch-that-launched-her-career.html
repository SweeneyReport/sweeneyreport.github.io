Sydney Sweeney's PowerPoint That Changed Her Life

Sydney Sweeney has said she wrote a five-year career plan at 12 and pitched it to her parents in a PowerPoint, before Euphoria made her a star.

By Sweeney Report · November 19, 2026

Long before Euphoria made her a household name, Sydney Sweeney made a pitch to the two people who needed convincing most: her parents. CNBC reported that the actor, who grew up in Spokane, Washington, wrote a five-year career plan at around age 12 and presented it to them as a PowerPoint. The family then spent years traveling between Spokane and Los Angeles for auditions.

A business pitch from a child

According to CNBC, which drew on an interview Sweeney gave to British GQ, the plan was laid out as a chain of steps. It reportedly included auditioning for a specific short film, being recruited by particular agents and then booking a film where she would meet a well-connected producer. Sweeney has framed the presentation as a way to show her parents that her dream of acting was serious and organized rather than a passing whim.

CNBC reported that her parents backed the idea. In the following years the family made repeated trips between Spokane and Los Angeles so she could audition.

The early years were not easy

The path was slower than the plan suggested. CNBC reported that Sweeney's early work included small roles on shows such as Grey's Anatomy and Criminal Minds, which fell short of what she had hoped for. During the same period, the outlet reported, her family lost their Spokane home because of financial difficulties, and her parents divorced.

Sweeney told British GQ that she had once believed she would earn enough by 18 to buy back her parents' old house, and that this might bring them back together, according to CNBC.

From small roles to Euphoria

CNBC reported that a few months after she turned 18, Sweeney booked the HBO miniseries Sharp Objects and then the Netflix series Everything Sucks!. She then won a role in HBO's Euphoria after a single audition tape, per the same report. The show became the breakout that put her in the public eye.

The PowerPoint story also drew wider attention. Business Insider and Yahoo covered the anecdote, with headlines suggesting fans could thank a slide deck for her Hollywood success.

What's next

Sweeney's story has become a popular example of early goal-setting in entertainment. The exact details of the original presentation have only been described in her own interviews, so they rest on her account. Her career has since expanded well beyond the plan she drew up as a child.

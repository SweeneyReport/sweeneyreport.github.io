Sydney Sweeney's Best and Worst Rotten Tomatoes Scores

From Reality at 93% to Madame Web at 10%, here is how critics have scored Sydney Sweeney's films on Rotten Tomatoes.

By Sweeney Report · November 4, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's film career has a wide spread of critic scores. According to her Rotten Tomatoes page, Reality (2023) is her highest-rated film at 93 percent. Madame Web (2024) is her lowest at 10 percent. These are Tomatometer scores, which reflect professional critics, not audience scores.

The top of the list

Reality leads her filmography on the site. Two 2019 titles, Big Time Adolescence and Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood, share second place at 86 percent each. Rotten Tomatoes also lists Dead Ant (2017) at 69 percent and Relentless (2017) at 68 percent.

Her more recent releases land in the middle of the range. The Housemaid (2025) sits at 74 percent. Immaculate (2024) is at 71 percent. Christy (2025) is at 66 percent, and Americana (2025) is at 63 percent.

The middle and the bottom

Anyone But You (2023) holds a 53 percent critics' score on her page. Coverage by outlets such as BGR has pointed out that the romantic comedy's audience score is much higher, at roughly 87 percent. That gap is a reminder that critics and viewers often respond differently.

Echo Valley (2025) is also at 53 percent, and Eden (2024) is at 58 percent. Further down are The Voyeurs (2021) at 45 percent and Night Teeth (2021) and Tell Me Your Name (2018), both at 38 percent.

At the very bottom is Madame Web, at 10 percent. Other snapshots of the page have shown it at slightly different figures, such as 11 percent, so the exact number has shifted over time.

Why the numbers move

Rotten Tomatoes scores are not fixed. They change as reviews are added, and early scores can swing sharply. Some early coverage of Reality, for instance, cited a perfect score based on only a handful of reviews. Other roundups have since listed it at 93 or 94 percent. A few of her titles also have no score yet, and The Housemaid's Secret, listed for 2027, has not been rated.

The list also covers only her films. It leaves out her television work, which critics have scored separately.

What's next

Sweeney has more projects on the way, including The Housemaid's Secret, so her scores may keep changing as new reviews come in. Which of her roles do you think deserves a higher score? Let us know in the comments on our YouTube Short.

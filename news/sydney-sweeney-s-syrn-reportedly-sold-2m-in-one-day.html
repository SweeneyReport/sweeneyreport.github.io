Sydney Sweeney's SYRN Reportedly Sold $2M in a Single Day

Page Six reportedly said SYRN's Birthday collection sold out in 24 hours, while its holiday ad drew online criticism. None of the figures are confirmed.

By Sweeney Report · October 31, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's lingerie brand SYRN reportedly sold more than $2 million of its new Birthday collection in a single day. Page Six, citing anonymous sources, reported that the line sold out within 24 hours. The brand's third-quarter sales reportedly doubled from the second quarter, and September was reportedly its biggest month. SYRN has not confirmed any of the figures.

The reported numbers

According to Page Six, the Birthday collection launched on 1 September, shortly before Sweeney's 29th birthday on 12 September. It belongs to SYRN's Romantic range. Fashion Times UK, which relayed the report on 28 October, put the sales at about £1.5 million.

The same sources reportedly said third-quarter sales were more than triple the brand's June sales. October was reportedly on track for a further 20 per cent increase over September. Fashion Times UK noted that stock shortages may have held back even higher sales, and that the figures have not been independently verified. Sweeney's representative did not immediately respond to the original outlet's request for comment.

Celebitchy, The Irish Sun and Reality Tea also picked up the story. Each framed it as a sign of growth for the young label.

Backlash over the holiday ad

The sales report arrived as SYRN faced criticism over its holiday advertisement, released in October. The campaign references Home Alone 2 and How the Grinch Stole Christmas. IBTimes UK reported that some online commenters objected to the use of Cindy Lou Who imagery to promote a lingerie brand.

Others argued that the character-inspired scenes were separate from the rest of the campaign. Fashion Times UK pointed out that the backlash reflects a public debate, but it does not show that criticism has affected sales.

Why it matters

If accurate, the reported results would suggest SYRN is turning attention around Sweeney into repeat business. Fashion Times UK said the sell-outs also point to a practical challenge, since demand only produces steady revenue when popular products stay in stock. Sources reportedly told Page Six that the company plans to widen its range and improve availability.

What's next

SYRN has not commented publicly, so the sales figures remain unconfirmed. Watch for an official statement from the brand, any restock or new product announcements, and whether the holiday campaign draws further reaction as the season approaches.

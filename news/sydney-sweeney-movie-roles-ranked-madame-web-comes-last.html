Sydney Sweeney's Movie Roles Ranked: Madame Web Lands Last

EarlyGame ranked 15 Sydney Sweeney film roles and put Madame Web at the bottom, with Cassidy Way, Anyone But You and The Voyeurs also on the list.

By Sweeney Report · November 18, 2026

Madame Web finished last in a new ranking of Sydney Sweeney's film work. The gaming and entertainment site EarlyGame published a list of her 15 feature film roles, ordered from worst to best, and placed the 2024 Sony Pictures release at number 15.

How the ranking worked

EarlyGame said the list covered feature films only. Sweeney's television roles, music videos and shorts were left out. The outlet wrote that it judged the performances themselves, so a hit's opening weekend or a glowing review counted for less than a single memorable scene, and even a weak film could contain one worth rewatching.

The bottom of the list

On Madame Web, EarlyGame argued that Sweeney's character, Julia Cornwall, was meant to be shy, but that almost every line was delivered at the same apologetic volume. According to the site, this made two very different situations feel alike: being stuck with two teenage strangers and being chased through New York by a man who wants her dead. EarlyGame added that glimpses of Julia in her Spider-Woman suit made the lack of personality more noticeable.

Americana, from 2023, took number 14. Cassidy Way, a 2016 kidnapping thriller from Red Thread Pictures, was number 13. EarlyGame said Sweeney's character, Kelsey Connors, gave away her unease too readily, and that the more interesting question of how Kelsey felt about her own family went unanswered.

Anyone But You and The Voyeurs

Anyone But You, the 2023 film with Glen Powell, was number 12. EarlyGame praised the scene involving a spider, saying Sweeney knew exactly how to play it. It also said the rhythm of the insult exchanges between her character, Bea, and Powell's Ben went slack. In the site's view, Sweeney often sounded as though she had prepared Bea's comebacks well in advance.

Big Time Adolescence, from 2019, was number 11, and The Voyeurs, from 2021, was number 10. EarlyGame described the latter as a film in which nobody was especially worried about subtlety. It said Sweeney captured the appeal of the first illicit look her character, Pippa, takes at the couple across the street. The excerpt reviewed for this article cut off before the rest of the ranking, so the top ten and the number one pick were not confirmed here.

What's next: The list is one outlet's opinion, and rankings like this often prompt debate among fans. EarlyGame's top ten, including its number one role, sits further up the gallery. Which of Sweeney's roles would you put first?

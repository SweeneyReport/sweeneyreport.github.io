Sydney Sweeney Backed by Former Bond Girl for 007 Role

Trina Parks, who appeared in Diamonds Are Forever, publicly backed Sydney Sweeney for a possible 007 part, though nothing has been confirmed.

By Sweeney Report · November 4, 2026

A former Bond girl has thrown her support behind Sydney Sweeney joining the 007 franchise. Trina Parks, an actor who appeared in Diamonds Are Forever, publicly said she would like to see Sweeney in a role in the next Bond film, according to The Express Tribune and NT News. Sweeney has not been cast, and no studio announcement has been made.

Who is backing her

The Express Tribune reported that Parks, a veteran of the Bond series, expressed support for the idea of Sweeney entering the franchise. NT News similarly reported that Parks endorsed the actor for a potential part in the upcoming film. Both outlets framed the comments as an opinion from a former cast member rather than inside information about the production.

Parks is part of the franchise's history through her work on Diamonds Are Forever, which gives her view some weight with longtime fans of the series. Still, her comments do not reflect any official decision by the filmmakers or the studio.

Where the next Bond film stands

The next Bond film is being directed by Denis Villeneuve. Casting for the project has been closely watched by fans and entertainment outlets, and many names have been floated in public discussion. Sweeney's has surfaced in that conversation, but there has been no confirmed link between her and the production in the reporting reviewed here.

It is worth separating the two things clearly. One is a public endorsement from a former franchise actor. The other is a casting decision, which is made by the production team and typically announced through official channels. At this stage, only the first has been reported.

Why it matters for Sweeney

Sweeney has built a high-profile career across film and television, and any link to a major franchise tends to draw attention. Support from a figure connected to the Bond series adds to the buzz around her name, even without any formal talks being reported. Neither The Express Tribune nor NT News reported that Sweeney or her representatives had commented on the matter.

What's next

For now, Sweeney is not officially attached to the Bond franchise, and Parks' backing remains one veteran's opinion. Any real development would need to come from the production or from Sweeney's team. Until then, it is best treated as speculation, and the Sweeney Report will update this story if an official announcement is made.

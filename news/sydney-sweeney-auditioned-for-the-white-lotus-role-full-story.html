Sydney Sweeney Had to Audition for The White Lotus

Sydney Sweeney has said she had to tape an audition and go through a callback to win Olivia Mossbacher in HBO's The White Lotus.

By Sweeney Report · November 6, 2026

Sydney Sweeney did not walk into The White Lotus. She has said she had to audition for the part of Olivia Mossbacher, despite her work on Euphoria. Mike White's HBO series premiered in July 2021, and the role later brought her an Emmy nomination for supporting actress in a limited series.

The audition

Sweeney has said that some people involved did not think she was right for the role because of Euphoria. Her response was to put herself on tape. She then went through a callback, as the other actors did. In coverage of her interviews, Marie Claire UK described the process as a fight for the part, and Bustle also reported on the audition.

One detail was reported secondhand. According to an account in the press, White reportedly did not recognize her from Euphoria when he watched her tape. These stories come from interviews and press retellings, not from casting records, so details may differ between outlets.

Emmy recognition

Olivia Mossbacher was a central part of the show's first season. The Mossbachers were a family on vacation at the resort, and Sweeney played the daughter. Her performance led to her first Emmy nomination, in the supporting actress in a limited series category.

The Television Academy lists her nominations under 2022. That year she was nominated twice, because she also received a nod for her work as Cassie Howard on Euphoria. Entertainment Tonight and other outlets noted at the time that she was a first-time double nominee. Connie Britton and Steve Zahn, who played her parents in The White Lotus, were also nominated.

Why it matters

The story shows how quickly Sweeney's career moved from television roles to the projects she is known for today. Casting a young actor who was already linked to a very different character can be difficult. Her account suggests she had to show she could play something else, and the Emmy recognition suggests the result impressed voters.

It is also a useful reminder that her best-known roles did not all come easily. She has described a similar path with Euphoria, where she has said she was initially told not to bother auditioning before she eventually sent in a tape and was booked.

What's next

Sweeney has continued to take on a wide range of film and television work since those nominations. Fans can expect more of her interviews to revisit how she won the parts that built her career.

Sydney Sweeney Defender Charley Hull Speaks Out on Her Big Shoot

Golfer Charley Hull, who backed Sydney Sweeney during the Novig ad backlash, spoke about her own Sports Illustrated Swimsuit shoot in Ibiza.

By Sweeney Report · November 19, 2026

Charley Hull, the English pro golfer who publicly defended Sydney Sweeney, has spoken about her own Sports Illustrated Swimsuit shoot. Fox News reported that Hull appeared at the Rolex LPGA Awards in Naples, Florida, on November 17. There she described a long day of work in Ibiza and said it gave her new respect for professional models.

Who is Charley Hull in the Sweeney story?

Hull first became part of the Sydney Sweeney conversation when she spoke up for the actress. Fox News reported that Hull backed Sweeney earlier, after the backlash over Sweeney's Novig advertisement. The outlet did not frame it as a feud. It presented Hull as an athlete who chose to weigh in on a public debate involving a high-profile celebrity.

That earlier support is the reason her comments at the awards event drew attention from entertainment and sports media alike. Yahoo Sports carried the Fox News story under a headline that described her as a defender of Sweeney.

What Hull said about the Ibiza shoot

According to Fox News, Hull described her Sports Illustrated Swimsuit shoot in Ibiza as a demanding experience. The report said the shoot ran from the morning until about eight p.m. It also said hair and makeup took up a large part of the schedule.

Her main takeaway, as relayed by the outlet, was admiration for the people who do this work for a living. Hull said, in essence, that she took her hat off to models because she had not realized how hard the job was. The comment was about the working day itself, not about any individual.

Why it matters

The story shows how a controversy around a celebrity advertising deal can draw in figures from other fields. Sweeney's Novig ad drew criticism, and Hull was one of the people who responded in her favor, according to Fox News. Others held different views, and those opinions belong to the people who expressed them.

It also shows how athletes now move between sports and fashion media. Hull's remarks gave a behind-the-scenes look at how long a major magazine shoot can take, which is a side of the industry that audiences rarely see.

What's next

What's next: Neither Hull nor Sweeney was reported to have announced anything further tied to these comments. Sweeney's representatives had not been quoted in the coverage reviewed. Whether more athletes weigh in when a celebrity faces public backlash remains an open question, and this site will report any new developments as they are confirmed.

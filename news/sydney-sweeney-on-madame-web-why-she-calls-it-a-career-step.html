Sydney Sweeney on Madame Web: What She Actually Said

Sydney Sweeney told GQ U.K. that Madame Web, a box-office flop, helped her build a relationship with Sony that led to Anyone But You and Barbarella.

By Sweeney Report · November 16, 2026

Sydney Sweeney described Madame Web as a calculated career move. In an interview with GQ U.K., as relayed by Deadline, she called the Sony superhero film a "building block" that helped her build a relationship with the studio. The film opened in the U.S. on February 14, 2024, and was a box-office disappointment, with a worldwide gross of roughly $100.5 million, according to Wikipedia's compilation of reported figures.

What she said

Sweeney said of the film: "To me, that film was a building block, it's what allowed me to build a relationship with Sony." She went on to link that relationship to two projects: "Because I did that, I was able to sell Anyone But You. I was able to get Barbarella." JoBlo noted that the comments were reported around March 2024.

She also addressed how little say an actor has over a film's fate. According to the same reporting, she said there was no outcome she could control on a film like that, especially as she was not a producer, and that you "sign up for whatever happens and take the ride."

The film's performance

Madame Web starred Dakota Johnson as Cassie Webb, with Sweeney as Julia Cornwall, one of three future Spider-Women. S. J. Clarkson directed it, in her first feature. Sony reported a net production budget of $80 million, though The Hollywood Reporter and Deadline reportedly put the real cost closer to the low $100 million range.

Reviews were harsh. Wikipedia lists a 10% Rotten Tomatoes score and a Metacritic score of 26 out of 100. The film also won three Golden Raspberry Awards, including Worst Picture. Sony had reportedly intended it to launch a new franchise, but those plans were reportedly dropped after the weak opening.

The projects she pointed to

Anyone But You, the romantic comedy Sweeney starred in, was reported to have been made for about $25 million and to have earned more than $200 million worldwide. Barbarella is a Sony remake in which she is attached to star and executive produce.

Dark Horizons, citing a June 2, 2026 Deadline report, said the remake was still moving forward after a long silence. It said Sweeney had formed a production company, Honey Trap, with a first-look deal at Sony. No release date has been announced, and director Edgar Wright reportedly described the project as still being at the script stage.

What's next

Whether Madame Web was a smart move is a matter of opinion. Sweeney's own account is that the film gave her access to Sony, and her ongoing deal with the studio suggests the relationship has lasted. The next test is whether Barbarella reaches production.

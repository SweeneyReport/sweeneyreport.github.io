Sydney Sweeney: $220M Hit vs $2M Flop, Same Star

Anyone But You earned about $220M worldwide on a $25M budget, while Christy opened to $1.3M and ended near $2M.

By Sweeney Report · November 14, 2026

Two Sydney Sweeney films have had very different runs at the box office. The romantic comedy Anyone But You (2023) grossed roughly $220 million worldwide, according to The Numbers. The boxing biopic Christy, released in November 2025, opened to about $1.3 million and finished with a worldwide total near $2 million.

The hit: Anyone But You

The Numbers lists a production budget of $25 million for Anyone But You. The film opened domestically to about $6 million. It kept drawing audiences afterward, reaching around $88 million in the United States and Canada, with the rest coming from international markets. The site's worldwide figure sits just under $220 million, roughly nine times the listed budget. Small variations appear between snapshots of the page.

The flop: Christy

Christy tells the story of boxer Christy Martin, with Sweeney in the title role. It debuted in about 2,000 theaters and took in roughly $1.3 million in its opening weekend, a result outlets such as GamesRadar and No Film School described as a very weak start. The Numbers puts its worldwide total at about $2.09 million, of which around $1.95 million came from the domestic market.

The budget was not officially announced, and estimates differ. The Numbers lists $15 million, while Dark Horizons reported that it was believed to fall in the $30 million to $40 million range. Neither figure is confirmed, so the exact loss is unclear.

Sweeney's response

On November 10, 2025, Sweeney addressed the results in an Instagram post, which TheWrap reported on. “We don’t always just make art for numbers; we make it for impact,” she wrote, according to the outlet. TheWrap said she described the film as meaningful to her and called it an honor to play Martin.

What’s next

The contrast shows how much a film’s genre, marketing and release strategy can matter, whatever the star’s name. Sweeney’s next releases will show whether her draw at the box office depends on the type of project.

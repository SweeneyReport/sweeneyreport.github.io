Sydney Sweeney Shares a Spooky Road Trip With Scooter Braun

Sydney Sweeney shared Instagram Stories of a fall road trip to Sleepy Hollow with Scooter Braun, including a playful Headless Horseman moment.

By Sweeney Report · November 7, 2026

Sydney Sweeney gave fans a look at a fall getaway with Scooter Braun on November 6. She posted a series of Instagram Stories from a road trip to Sleepy Hollow, New York, Geo News reported. One clip showed the 45-year-old music producer joking around as the Headless Horseman while she laughed behind the camera.

What she shared from Sleepy Hollow

According to Geo News, the Stories began with a few hints about where the couple was headed. The News International reported that Sweeney, 29, also posted a picture of a bridge and asked her followers whether they recognized the spot.

In the funniest clip, Braun pulled his head inside his jacket and raised his arms, apparently imitating the Headless Horseman from the Sleepy Hollow legend. Sweeney's laughter could be heard in the background, Geo News said. She captioned the video "Hint..."

She then revealed the location with a photo of a sign reading "Welcome to Historic Sleepy Hollow, Settled in 1640." Her next caption read "Little road trip," according to Geo News.

How the couple got here

Geo News reported that Sweeney and Braun were first linked in Italy after Jeff Bezos and Lauren Sánchez's wedding in June 2025. The outlet also said the pair began dating casually that month. Neither outlet's report in this story included a public statement from either of them about the relationship, and the Instagram posts were the main new material.

The trip comes as Sweeney keeps offering occasional glimpses of their time together on her social media accounts.

Reports of a possible move

The News International noted that the Stories followed a separate report from The Sun. That report cited an unnamed source who said Sweeney was reportedly looking at a quieter place to live with Braun, possibly in the Cotswolds in England. The source claimed she had visited the area twice in the past 10 months and had viewed property there in recent months. The Sun's claims are unconfirmed, and neither Sweeney nor Braun has publicly commented on them in the coverage reviewed.

What's next

Sweeney has not said whether more trips with Braun will appear on her page. For now, the Sleepy Hollow Stories are the latest public sign of the relationship. Any confirmation of a move or other plans would have to come from the couple or their representatives.

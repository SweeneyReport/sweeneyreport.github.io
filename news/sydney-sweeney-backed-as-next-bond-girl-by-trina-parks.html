Sydney Sweeney Just Got a Bond Girl's Seal of Approval

Diamonds Are Forever actor Trina Parks told The Telegraph she would love to see Sydney Sweeney as the next Bond love interest.

By Sweeney Report · November 4, 2026

Trina Parks, the actor who played Thumper in Diamonds Are Forever, has publicly backed Sydney Sweeney for the next Bond girl. Parks reportedly made the comments to The Telegraph, saying Sweeney would do a "wonderful" job. Yahoo, Page Six, Daily Times and The News International all picked up the story. No casting has been confirmed, and Sweeney has not responded.

Who is Trina Parks?

Parks appeared opposite Sean Connery in Diamonds Are Forever, released in 1971. She played Thumper, and she is widely described as the first Black Bond girl in the franchise's history. Her comments carry some weight with fans because she worked on the series during its early decades.

The casting talk

The Telegraph also reportedly wrote that Sweeney is among the names being discussed for the role, alongside Margaret Qualley and Zendaya. The report did not amount to an official shortlist from the producers, and the outlets covering it presented the names as part of ongoing speculation rather than confirmed auditions.

Nobody has been officially cast in the next Bond film. Sweeney herself has not commented on the Telegraph report or on Parks's remarks, and no representative has confirmed any talks.

Why it matters

Support from a former Bond actor adds to the public conversation around the franchise's next chapter, which has drawn constant attention from fans and entertainment media. For Sweeney, the mention places her in the same discussion as other high-profile names at a time when her film and television career is widely followed. Still, attention from a veteran of the series is not the same as interest from the studio, and the speculation should be read as such.

Reaction from fans has been mixed in the coverage reviewed, with some welcoming the idea and others saying they would rather wait for official news. Those views came from commentary around the story, not from any formal announcement.

What's next

Nothing is confirmed. Viewers will need to wait for an official casting announcement from the Bond producers, or for any statement from Sweeney or her team, before treating the rumor as more than talk.

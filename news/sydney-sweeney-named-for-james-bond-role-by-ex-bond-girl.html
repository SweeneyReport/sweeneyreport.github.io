Sydney Sweeney Named for Bond by Ex-Bond Girl, Per Page Six

Page Six reported that Brad Pitt, Florence Pugh and Sydney Sweeney are linked to the new James Bond film, though no casting has been announced.

By Sweeney Report · November 18, 2026

Sydney Sweeney was named as a possible addition to the next James Bond film by a former Bond girl, Page Six reported on November 17, 2026. The outlet said Sweeney, Brad Pitt and Florence Pugh are all vying to join the highly anticipated new movie, which Page Six described as starring Robert Pattinson. No casting has been officially announced, and Sweeney herself was not quoted in the report.

Three names, one franchise

Page Six published a video segment about the buzz around the project. According to the outlet, Pitt, Sweeney and Pugh are all hoping to land a part. The report framed the three as competing for a place in the film, though it did not say that any of them had been offered a role or had formally auditioned.

Pugh was the most direct of the group. Page Six reported that she revealed she had texted the film's director, asking to play a villain. The outlet did not say whether the director replied.

Brad Pitt and the ex-Bond girl

Pitt was also asked whether he would join the production. Page Six said his answer appears in its full video, and the written summary did not spell out what he said. For that reason, his position on the film should be treated as unknown until the clip is viewed or he speaks about it again.

The segment also said a former Bond girl picked Sweeney as the star she would like to see in the franchise. Page Six did not name her in the summary available, so it is unclear which Bond film she appeared in or how she explained her choice. Her pick amounts to an opinion rather than an insider claim about the studio's plans.

What it means for Sweeney

For Sweeney, the mention is a sign of how often her name comes up in casting conversations for big franchises. It is not evidence of talks with the producers. Nothing in the Page Six report suggested she had been approached about the film, and the outlet's wording described what the actors reportedly want rather than what the studio has decided.

What's next: The Bond team has not announced any casting beyond what has already been reported, so these names remain speculation for now. Any official confirmation would likely come from the studio or the filmmakers. Until then, Page Six's video is the main source for the claims about Pitt, Pugh and Sweeney.

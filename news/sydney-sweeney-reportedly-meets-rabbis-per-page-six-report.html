Sydney Sweeney Meeting With Rabbis? What Page Six Reports

Page Six reportedly says Sydney Sweeney has met with rabbis and may be considering conversion, claims that remain unconfirmed by her or Scooter Braun.

By Sweeney Report · November 15, 2026

Sydney Sweeney has reportedly been meeting with rabbis, according to a Page Six report that was picked up by The Hollywood Gossip and Inshorts. Sources told the outlet she may be considering converting to Judaism, and that music executive Scooter Braun reportedly plans to propose within the next six months. Neither Sweeney nor Braun has publicly confirmed any of it.

What Page Six reported

Page Six cited unnamed insiders who said the actress had held meetings with rabbis in recent weeks. The outlet's sources reportedly linked this to the couple's possible plans to marry. One source said the pair was discussing starting a family, and that Braun felt strongly about the subject, according to the report.

Another insider told Page Six that Braun was planning to propose “within the next six months.” A further source described him as determined to marry her, according to the outlet. Page Six also reported that witnesses saw the two at Rosh Hashanah services at Temple Sholom in Greenwich, Connecticut.

Background on the couple

The Hollywood Gossip noted that Braun, a father of three from his first marriage, reportedly once compared Sweeney to Marilyn Monroe. It pointed out that Monroe converted to Judaism before marrying playwright Arthur Miller. That comparison was offered by the outlet as context, not as evidence of Sweeney's plans.

The site also said the two recently traveled together to Lake Powell, Utah. It added that Braun posted a public tribute to Sweeney on her birthday last month, writing that she made him “smile harder” and made “the future feel like something I can’t wait to get to.”

How reliable is the report?

The claims rest entirely on anonymous sources, and neither Sweeney nor her representatives had addressed them in the coverage reviewed. Religious conversion is a deeply personal matter, and the reported details about marriage and children are secondhand. Readers should treat them as unverified. The Hollywood Gossip's own framing was a question rather than a confirmation of her plans.

What’s next: Unless Sweeney or Braun comments, the story will likely stay at the level of reported speculation. Any engagement announcement, if one happens, would be the first real confirmation of the relationship’s direction. Until then, the Page Six account remains unconfirmed.

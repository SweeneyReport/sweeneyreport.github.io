Sydney Sweeney's Housemaid Made 10x Its Budget, Beating Anyone But You

The Housemaid grossed about $400 million on a $35 million budget, passing Anyone But You as Sydney Sweeney's biggest starring hit. A sequel is on the way.

By Sweeney Report · November 15, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's thriller The Housemaid has grossed about $400 million worldwide on a reported budget of $35 million, which makes it her biggest box office hit as a lead. Directed by Paul Feig and released by Lionsgate, the film opened on December 19, 2025. Box Office Mojo lists roughly $126.4 million in North America and $274.1 million overseas, for a worldwide total of about $400.5 million.

How it passed Anyone But You

Before this film, Sweeney's biggest starring success was the romantic comedy Anyone But You, which earned about $220 million worldwide. Slashfilm reported that The Housemaid overtook that total while it was still in theaters. The site added that Anyone But You had been the first romantic comedy in six years to cross $200 million.

The Housemaid's return on its budget is much larger than that of her earlier hit. A reported $35 million cost against a gross of about $400 million is more than ten times over. Anyone But You had a budget of about $25 million, which is roughly nine times over by the same measure.

The run began modestly. Box Office Mojo shows a domestic opening weekend of about $19 million. Bol News reported that the film had earned about $133 million worldwide after just over two weeks. It then kept building, and by March 2026 the worldwide total was reported at nearly $400 million.

A tough year, then a breakout

Slashfilm noted that the success followed a difficult stretch for Sweeney's other 2025 releases. According to the outlet, Americana made about $500,000 in its opening weekend, Eden made $2.8 million against a reported $35 million budget, and Christy made about $2 million in total. Echo Valley went straight to Apple TV+ rather than playing in theaters. Against that record, The Housemaid stood out sharply.

The sequel

Lionsgate moved quickly to develop a follow-up. Bol News reported in January 2026 that The Housemaid's Secret was in the works, with Feig and Sweeney expected to return and Rebecca Sonnenshine writing the script. The Wrap and Variety later reported that Kirsten Dunst had joined the cast. Lionsgate's announcement described her character as Wendy Garrick. Michele Morrone is also set to return.

Release timing has been reported inconsistently. Some outlets cited a December 2027 date, but that was not confirmed by a primary source at the time of writing.

What's next

Attention now turns to The Housemaid's Secret and to whether Sweeney can repeat this result with her next projects. For now, The Housemaid is her highest-grossing film as a lead, and it is one of the most profitable releases of its year for Lionsgate. Which of her box office results surprised you most?

Sydney Sweeney's Sleepy Hollow Movie Reportedly Lands Its Ichabod

Nicholas Galitzine is reportedly in final negotiations to play Ichabod Crane opposite Sydney Sweeney's Katrina Van Tassel in Sony's Sleepy Hollow reimagining, Hollow.

By Sweeney Report · November 6, 2026

Nicholas Galitzine is reportedly in final negotiations to play Ichabod Crane in Hollow, Sony's upcoming reimagining of The Legend of Sleepy Hollow, according to Deadline. Sydney Sweeney is attached to star as Katrina Van Tassel. Galitzine is best known for Masters of the Universe, in which he played He-Man. No deal had been officially confirmed at the time of the reports.

The story behind Hollow

The film is based on Hollow, a novel by Lindsey Anderson Beer that is scheduled for release in mid-2027, as reported by Creepy Catalog. Deadline described the book as a retelling of Washington Irving's classic tale. Unlike the original, the story is told from the perspective of Katrina Van Tassel, the wealthy farmer's daughter who is pursued by both Ichabod Crane and Abraham Van Brunt.

Beer is set to write and direct the adaptation of her own novel, according to Creepy Catalog's account of the announcement.

The cast so far

Sweeney, known for Euphoria, had already been announced in the lead role. Creepy Catalog also reported that Belmont Cameli, whose credits include Off Campus, had been announced for the cast. If Galitzine's deal closes, he would take on the role of the schoolteacher whose arrival in Sleepy Hollow sets the central love triangle in motion.

The Playlist, Comic Basics and IMDb News all carried the report, each describing Galitzine as in talks or nearing a deal. Because the negotiations have not been formally announced by the studio, the casting should be considered unconfirmed for now.

Why it matters

The project pairs Sweeney with an actor who has recently moved into big franchise territory, while keeping the focus on a character, Katrina, who is often a supporting figure in earlier versions of the tale. Reimagining a well-known public-domain story from a new point of view is a growing approach in genre filmmaking, and casting will be an early signal of the tone Sony is aiming for.

What's next: Watch for official confirmation of Galitzine's casting from Sony or his representatives, along with further cast announcements. Beer's novel is expected in mid-2027, and no release date for the film had been reported.

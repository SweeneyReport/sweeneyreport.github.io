Sydney Sweeney's The Housemaid: From Bestselling Novel to Hit

Paul Feig's thriller, adapted from Freida McFadden's novel, grew from a $35 million production into a global hit, and Lionsgate has ordered a sequel.

By Sweeney Report · November 14, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's thriller The Housemaid has passed $300 million at the worldwide box office. The film is based on Freida McFadden's bestselling novel and was directed by Paul Feig for Lionsgate. It reached U.S. theaters in December 2025 and cost a reported $35 million to make. Lionsgate has since greenlit a sequel.

From bestselling book to screen

The film adapts McFadden's 2022 novel. Rebecca Sonnenshine wrote the screenplay. Sweeney plays Millie Calloway, a young woman who takes a live-in housekeeping job with a wealthy family. Amanda Seyfried plays Nina Winchester, the lady of the house. Brandon Sklenar, Michele Morrone and Elizabeth Perkins also appear in the cast, according to Wikipedia's film entry.

The film premiered in New York on December 2, 2025, and Lionsgate released it in the U.S. on December 19. It arrived on home video in early 2026.

Box office and reception

Early reports showed a strong run. ARY News reported that the film passed $300 million worldwide at the end of January 2026. Its total is reportedly higher still. Wikipedia lists a worldwide gross of about $400 million, including roughly $126 million from the U.S. and Canada. Those figures are tracker-based and may change.

Critics were fairly positive. Wikipedia's summary of review aggregators lists a 74% score on Rotten Tomatoes and a 65 on Metacritic. Audiences polled by CinemaScore gave it a "B." Seyfried's performance drew particular praise. Many reviewers described the film as a pulpy thriller in the style of the 1990s.

The sequel

Lionsgate confirmed a follow-up called The Housemaid's Secret. Bloody Disgusting reported that the studio was gearing up for a 2026 production start. The sequel adapts the second novel in McFadden's series. Feig is returning as director, and Sweeney is set to return as the lead. She is also reportedly an executive producer.

Wikipedia says Morrone is back and that Kirsten Dunst has been added to the cast. It also says principal photography reportedly began in October 2026 in Hoboken, New Jersey. It lists a release date of December 17, 2027. Reports differ on how large a role Seyfried will have, since her character is not central to the second book.

What's next

With the sequel in production and a December 2027 release date reported, The Housemaid has become a franchise for Sweeney. It is also another sign of how bestselling thrillers can become hits at a modest budget. Studios may now look at other popular novels as adaptation candidates.

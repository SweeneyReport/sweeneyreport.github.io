Sydney Sweeney's Hidden Decade of Roles Before Euphoria

Years before Cassie in Euphoria, Sydney Sweeney built a TV résumé of guest spots and 2018 roles on Netflix, Hulu and HBO.

By Sweeney Report · October 28, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's breakout as Cassie Howard in HBO's Euphoria in 2019 came after roughly ten years of screen work. According to her Wikipedia filmography, she made her television debut in 2009 and went on to guest-star in network series. In 2018 she held three notable roles: Emaline Addario in Netflix's Everything Sucks!, Eden Spencer in The Handmaid's Tale, and Alice in HBO's Sharp Objects.

The early years

Wikipedia's account of her career lists a small part in an episode of Heroes in 2009 as her television debut, along with an appearance in Criminal Minds. Her film debut, the horror comedy ZMD: Zombies of Mass Destruction, followed in 2010, and she appeared in 90210 the same year. The same source says she received two Young Artist Award nominations for her work in the short film Takeo and the series Chase.

Later guest and supporting parts included Grey's Anatomy in 2014, followed by The Middle and Pretty Little Liars in 2017, according to the same listing.

The 2018 turning point

Wikipedia describes 2018 as the year her profile rose. She starred in Everything Sucks!, a Netflix series set in Oregon in 1996, as Emaline Addario. Britannica says her career gained momentum once she was cast in that role.

She also had a recurring role as Eden Spencer in the second season of The Handmaid's Tale. Bustle reported that this debut came only a couple of months after Everything Sucks! was released.

On HBO's miniseries Sharp Objects, she played Alice in a recurring part. Wikipedia notes that she filmed it at the same time as Everything Sucks!, so she worked on both productions during the same period.

Cassie and beyond

Euphoria premiered on HBO in June 2019, with Sweeney playing Cassie Howard. Wikipedia describes the part as her breakout role. That same year she also appeared in Quentin Tarantino's Once Upon a Time in Hollywood.

Looking back, the sequence of roles shows a steady build, from small guest appearances to a lead part on a streaming series and a role opposite Amy Adams on Sharp Objects, before the attention that followed Euphoria.

Sydney Sweeney's Car Hobby Is Becoming a Business: Syd's Garage

Backfire News reported a trademark filing for Syd's Garage, a name from Sydney Sweeney's TikTok that may point to a bigger car-focused business.

By Sweeney Report · October 26, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's passion for classic cars may be growing into a business. Backfire News reported that a trademark was reportedly filed for the name Syd's Garage, a brand that grew out of her car-focused content online. The filing has not been confirmed by Sweeney or her team in the material reviewed, so its purpose remains unclear.

Where the name came from

According to Backfire News, "Syd's Garage" is the name of Sweeney's TikTok account. On that account she shared a restoration project involving a Ford Bronco, giving followers a look at her hands-on interest in vehicles. The outlet presented the trademark as the next step in a hobby that has steadily moved from personal passion to public project.

The same report pointed to her own 1965 Ford Mustang, which she nicknamed Britney. Backfire News said that car inspired a custom Ford Mustang GT created for the model's sixtieth anniversary. That collaboration tied her personal vehicle directly to a special edition from one of the best-known names in the car industry.

Ford and Dickies already on board

Backfire News also noted that Ford and the workwear company Dickies have already released Syd's Garage workwear. That means the name has already appeared on products connected to major brands, which is part of why the reported trademark filing drew attention. A trademark generally protects a name for use on specific goods or services, though the report did not detail what categories the filing covers.

It is worth separating what has been confirmed from what has not. The workwear release and the Mustang project were described as having already happened. The trademark, by contrast, was described only as reportedly filed, and no launch plans were attached to it in the report.

Why it matters

Sweeney has built a public profile through film and television, and she has also expanded into business ventures and brand partnerships. A garage-themed brand would add a different kind of project to that list, rooted in an interest she has already shown publicly rather than in her acting work. Backfire News framed the story as an example of a hobby becoming a business, and the combination of a social media following, a custom Ford and a workwear line gives that idea some context.

What's next

For now, there has been no official word on what Syd's Garage might become beyond the existing workwear. Any announcement from Sweeney, Ford or Dickies would clarify whether the trademark points to a larger product line, more collaborations or something else entirely. Until then, the filing remains a report worth watching.

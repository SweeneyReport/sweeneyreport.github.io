Sydney Sweeney's Housemaid's Secret Set Video From E! News

E! News posted a clip of Sydney Sweeney taking a selfie with a dog on the set of The Housemaid's Secret, the sequel Variety said opens in December 2027.

By Sweeney Report · November 19, 2026

E! News has published a new video from the set of The Housemaid's Secret, showing Sydney Sweeney taking a selfie with a dog. The clip is available on the outlet's YouTube channel. Variety reported that the sequel is scheduled to reach theaters on December 17, 2027.

What the video shows

The E! News upload is titled "Sydney Sweeney takes a selfie with a dog on the set of 'The Housemaid's Secret.'" That title is the only description of the content available. The video's description section on YouTube lists only the channel's usual hashtags and a general line about its entertainment coverage.

Because the outlet gave no further context, this report does not speculate about where or when the footage was filmed, who else appears in it, or whose dog is in the clip. Anyone curious can watch it directly on the E! News channel.

What is known about the sequel

Variety reported that The Housemaid's Secret has a theatrical release date of December 17, 2027. The trade publication also reported that Paul Feig is returning to direct. Feig directed the first film, so his return gives the follow-up some continuity behind the camera.

Variety additionally reported that Kirsten Dunst has joined the cast. That detail comes from the report and was not independently confirmed here, so it should be read as reportedly accurate until the studio or the actor says more. Sweeney's own involvement in the sequel is shown by the set footage that E! News published.

Why it matters

Set videos like this one are a common way for entertainment outlets to mark progress on a film before any official marketing begins. A short, light clip does not reveal plot or character details, and none were claimed in this case. It does show that the production is active and that the press is following it.

The release date is still more than a year away, which leaves a long stretch for additional casting news, photos and a first trailer. No trailer or plot synopsis had been mentioned in the sources reviewed for this article.

What's next

Nothing further has been announced beyond the details above. Updates on The Housemaid's Secret, including any official confirmation of the casting report or a first look, will be covered here as they are published by Variety, E! News and other outlets.

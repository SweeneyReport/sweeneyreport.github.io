Sydney Sweeney Loses Top Spot in Bond Girl Odds to Ex Co-Star

Margaret Qualley now leads the next Bond girl betting odds at 33 percent, per BetOnline, with Sydney Sweeney at 25 percent, tied with Anya Taylor-Joy.

By Sweeney Report · October 27, 2026

Sydney Sweeney is no longer the favorite in the betting odds for the next Bond girl. Comic Book Resources reported, citing The Hollywood Reporter, that BetOnline's latest numbers put Margaret Qualley in first place at 33 percent, while Sweeney dropped to 25 percent, tied for second with Anya Taylor-Joy.

The updated odds

According to the figures shared by BetOnline, Florence Pugh sits fourth at 17 percent, followed by Zendaya at 13 percent. Lauren Sánchez was listed at 5 percent and Kate Winslet at 4 percent. Comic Book Resources noted that Margot Robbie and Scarlett Johansson also appeared on the list, but much lower, at roughly 3 percent each.

Comic Book Resources said Sweeney had held the top spot throughout the lengthy casting process for the next James Bond film. The shift arrived shortly before the new 007 was expected to be officially chosen, according to the outlet. Betting odds reflect wagering activity and are not confirmation of any casting decision.

A shared history with Qualley

The new frontrunner is not a stranger to Sweeney. Comic Book Resources reported that the two appeared together in Quentin Tarantino's Once Upon a Time in Hollywood in 2019, where both played members of the Manson Family.

Qualley's profile has grown since then. The outlet pointed to her role in the 2024 horror film The Substance, which earned her a Golden Globe nomination. It also noted that she recently appeared in Ridley Scott's The Dog Stars alongside Jacob Elordi, who was Sweeney's co-star on Euphoria.

What the odds mean for Sweeney

Sweeney remains one of the leading names in the market, level with Taylor-Joy and only eight points behind Qualley. Odds of this kind tend to move as rumors circulate and bettors react, and neither Sweeney nor Qualley has been confirmed for any Bond role in the reporting reviewed.

What's next: The official announcement of the new 007 is the next milestone in the casting process. Once that happens, attention is likely to turn to the actors who might join the lead, and the odds may shift again.

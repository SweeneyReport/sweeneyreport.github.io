Sydney Sweeney Explains Why She Really Took Madame Web

Sydney Sweeney reportedly told GQ UK that Madame Web was a business decision that helped her build a relationship with Sony.

By Sweeney Report · October 29, 2026

Sydney Sweeney has reportedly described her decision to join Madame Web as a calculated career move. IMDb reported that the actor called the Sony film a "strategic business decision" in an interview with GQ UK. She reportedly said the project helped her build a relationship with the studio, and she credited that relationship with helping her make the romantic comedy Anyone But You.

The background

Madame Web was Sony's Marvel-adjacent superhero film and starred Dakota Johnson in the title role. Sweeney's casting drew attention well before the film came out. In August 2023, Dexerto reported that she would play Spider-Woman, though it noted that other rumors pointed to a different role for her. Dexerto framed that as a report, not a confirmed detail.

The finished film was panned by critics and stumbled at the box office. That reception is the context for Sweeney's remarks: she was asked why she took a project that performed so poorly.

The Anyone But You connection

According to the IMDb summary of the interview, Sweeney linked her time with Sony directly to Anyone But You, in which she starred opposite Glen Powell. By her account, the goodwill built through Madame Web helped make that film possible. We could not confirm the exact wording of her comments, so they are best read as the outlets' account of the GQ UK interview.

The comparison is hard to ignore. One film was criticized and struggled commercially, while the other is the romantic comedy she credits to the relationship the first one helped create. Sweeney's framing suggests she weighed a studio partnership more heavily than the reception of any single title.

A smart move or a gamble?

Reaction to her comments will likely depend on how people measure success. Some may see a shrewd, long-term strategy in which one project opens the door to another. Others may argue that attaching her name to a heavily criticized film carried real reputational risk. Neither the IMDb report nor the sources we reviewed included reactions from Sony or from Sweeney's representatives.

Her candor also stands out. Actors often describe their choices in terms of the script or the creative team. Sweeney instead described the role in plain business terms, in line with her work as a producer and entrepreneur.

What's next

Sweeney has not been reported to have said whether she would work with Sony again. For now, her comments add a new layer to the long-running conversation about Madame Web and about how she chooses her projects. We will update this story if the full GQ UK interview or any response from the studio adds detail.

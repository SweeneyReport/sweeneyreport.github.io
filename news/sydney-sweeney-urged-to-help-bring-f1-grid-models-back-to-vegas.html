Sydney Sweeney Asked to Bring Back F1 Grid Models in Vegas

A former grid girl publicly asked Sydney Sweeney to back the return of models to the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix, according to Fox News and Marca.

By Sweeney Report · November 14, 2026

A former Formula 1 grid girl has made a public appeal to Sydney Sweeney, asking the actress to help bring models back to the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix. Fox News and Marca both reported the appeal in the days before the race weekend. No response from Sweeney had been reported at the time of writing.

What was reported

Fox News, in a piece on its OutKick sports section, described the former grid girl as wanting Sweeney to push for models to return to the track ahead of the Las Vegas race. Marca, the Spanish sports outlet, ran its own English-language report on the same appeal, saying the former grid girl was calling on Sweeney to bring the models back. The outlets framed the request as a direct message to the actress. Neither report indicated that Sweeney had any formal role with Formula 1 or the Las Vegas event, and nothing in the coverage suggested she had been in contact with the sport about the idea.

Why grid models are a talking point

Formula 1 ended the long-running grid girl tradition ahead of the 2018 season. Since then, the starting grid has looked different on race day. The decision drew a mix of reactions at the time, and the subject has resurfaced in fan debates in the years since.

The Las Vegas Grand Prix has become one of the most visible stops on the calendar. It is run on the Strip at night, and the event leans heavily on spectacle and entertainment. That setting is part of why the former grid girl's request found an audience, as the race is often presented as a showcase for the sport's glamour side.

Sweeney's position

Sweeney has not publicly addressed the appeal, based on the reporting available. There was no indication in the Fox News or Marca coverage that her representatives had commented. It is also unclear why the former grid girl chose to address Sweeney specifically, and the outlets did not report any prior connection between the actress and Formula 1's grid arrangements.

Any decision on whether models return to the grid would rest with Formula 1 and the race organizers, not with an outside public figure.

What's next: The Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend will show whether the appeal gains traction. Formula 1 has not been reported to have changed its position on grid models, and Sweeney has not responded. Further coverage will depend on whether either side comments.

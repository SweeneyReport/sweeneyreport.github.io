Sydney Sweeney's Housemaid Sequel Shoot Pays Hoboken Big

Sydney Sweeney and Michele Morrone returned to Hoboken to film The Housemaid's Secret, a shoot the city says will bring in $36,100.

By Sweeney Report · November 19, 2026

Sydney Sweeney and Michele Morrone returned to Hoboken, New Jersey, this week to film additional scenes for The Housemaid's Secret, and the city says the shoot will generate $36,100 in revenue. Patch reported that the two actors were seen filming Wednesday morning at a brownstone in the uptown part of the city, with notices posted nearby indicating the crew would stay through Thursday.

What the city says it earns

Patch reported that a city spokesperson put the revenue from the new shoot at $36,100. The same outlet said the city received about $22,000 from the production company for its earlier filming in town last month. According to the spokesperson, Hoboken has taken in roughly $300,000 from filming so far this year.

The shoot itself

Patch reported that the film is directed by Paul Feig and serves as a sequel to the 2025 movie The Housemaid. The first film follows a former prison inmate who takes a housekeeping job with a wealthy Long Island family in order to start over. In an earlier report, Patch listed Kirsten Dunst alongside Sweeney and Morrone as stars of the sequel.

The production had previously filmed in Hoboken on October 14 and 15, near and inside a coffee shop called The Hive, according to Patch. The outlet said Sweeney earned praise at that time for taking photos with local residents.

For this week's work, Patch said a posted neighborhood notice listed shooting hours of about 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Wednesday and 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Thursday. The earlier Patch article said the crew planned to film on Hudson and Washington streets between 11th and 13th Streets, and that no-parking signs were put up ahead of time. Patch also reported that the production said it works with the city, the Hoboken Police Department and the New Jersey Film Commission to obtain the necessary permits.

Patch said several cast members were seen wearing cowboy hats near a new street light prop. A crew member told the outlet he could not say which cast members would be on set this week.

What's next

No release date for The Housemaid's Secret was mentioned in Patch's reporting. With filming set to wrap up in Hoboken after Thursday, further details on the production are expected to come from the studio or the filmmakers.

Sydney Sweeney and Scooter Braun's Road Trip to Sleepy Hollow

OK Magazine published photos of Sydney Sweeney and Scooter Braun on what it called a little road trip to Sleepy Hollow.

By Sweeney Report · November 13, 2026

Sydney Sweeney and Scooter Braun were photographed together on a getaway to Sleepy Hollow, according to OK Magazine. The outlet published pictures of the couple and described the outing as a "little road trip." Braun is a music executive, and the two have been seen together on a number of occasions this year.

What OK Magazine reported

OK Magazine's photo report focused on the couple's day trip to the New York town best known for its spooky folklore. The outlet framed the excursion as a relaxed, low-key outing rather than a formal public appearance. Neither Sweeney nor Braun was reported to have commented on the photos, and the details of the trip come from the magazine's account alone.

OK Magazine referred to Braun as her boyfriend. As the relationship is described in that outlet's coverage, this article treats the characterization as the magazine's own.

A year of shared outings

The Sleepy Hollow trip is only the latest in a series of appearances together. In May, the pair reportedly rode electric bikes through Times Square to see Sweeney's American Eagle billboard. That campaign is one of her best-known endorsement deals, and the reported outing placed Braun alongside her at a moment tied directly to her work.

Sweeney also shared a photo carousel from a trip to Australia that included Braun. Posting those images on her own account was one of the more visible ways the two have appeared together, as opposed to being photographed by outside outlets.

Why it matters

Sweeney's personal life draws steady press attention alongside her film, television and business projects. Braun's background in the music industry adds to the interest in their pairing, though neither has publicly detailed the relationship in the coverage cited here. Reports like OK Magazine's tend to rely on photographs and observed outings, so readers should treat any claims beyond what the images show as unconfirmed.

What's next

Neither Sweeney nor Braun has announced any upcoming plans together, and no statement about the Sleepy Hollow trip had been reported at the time of writing. Further sightings or social media posts from either of them would likely be the next source of news on the subject. The Sweeney Report will update this story if either side comments.

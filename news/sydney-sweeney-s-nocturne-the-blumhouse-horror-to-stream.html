Sydney Sweeney's Forgotten Blumhouse Horror You Can Stream

Sydney Sweeney's 2020 Blumhouse thriller Nocturne premiered on Prime Video, and many fans overlooked it. Here is what to know before you watch.

By Sweeney Report · November 17, 2026

Sydney Sweeney led Nocturne, a supernatural horror film that premiered on Amazon Prime Video on October 13, 2020. It was part of the Welcome to the Blumhouse anthology, and many of her fans never watched it. Sweeney plays Juliet, a piano student whose twin sister Vivian is played by Madison Iseman. Zu Quirke wrote and directed the film, which was her feature directing debut.

What the film is about

According to its Wikipedia entry, Juliet and Vivian Lowe are final-year piano students at an elite performing arts school. Vivian is the prodigy and has already been accepted to Juilliard. Juliet feels overshadowed and takes a gap year. After a student's death, both sisters compete for a place in the senior showcase. Juliet then becomes absorbed in the dead student's notebook, and her hold on reality begins to loosen.

Jacques Colimon, Ivan Shaw and Julie Benz are also in the cast. Jason Blum's Blumhouse Television produced the film, and Amazon Studios distributed it. It was the fourth film in the eight-film anthology. Its runtime is about 90 minutes.

How critics responded

Reviews were mixed. Wikipedia lists a 62% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes from 58 reviews, and a Metacritic score of 58 out of 100 from 11 critics. The Hollywood Reporter described the film as familiar and short on suspense, although it credited the two leads with generating some tension. RogerEbert.com took a more favorable view and reportedly called it the strongest entry in the anthology. IndieWire, as summarized on Wikipedia, praised Sweeney's performance but compared the film unfavorably to Black Swan.

A correction on Along for the Ride

The Short that accompanies this article includes a correction about Netflix's Along for the Ride, released May 6, 2022. The cast listings reviewed for this article, including Netflix's own cast guide, name Emma Pasarow, Belmont Cameli, Kate Bosworth, Dermot Mulroney and Andie MacDowell. They do not name Sweeney. Anyone looking for her work from that period should watch Nocturne instead.

What's next

Sydney Sweeney's SYRN Spotlights a New Model, Gabriella Couto

Sydney Sweeney's SYRN lingerie label tagged model Gabriella Couto by name in a new campaign called Bite, The Sun reported.

By Sweeney Report · November 20, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's lingerie label SYRN put a new model in the spotlight this month. The Sun reported that the brand posted a campaign featuring model Gabriella Couto, and that SYRN tagged Couto by name in the caption. The set was named Bite, after its color, according to the outlet.

What was reported

The Sun covered the campaign in November 2026, and TheRichest followed with its own write-up based on that report. Both outlets said SYRN credited Couto directly in its Instagram post, and TheRichest added that the caption included a red heart emoji.

The coverage framed Couto as a new face for the label. Neither outlet, in the material reviewed, reported a formal announcement from SYRN about a long-term role for her, so the extent of her work with the brand remains unclear.

Background: SYRN and its founder

Sweeney launched SYRN as her own intimate apparel label. According to TheRichest, she regularly models its pieces herself, which has made her the most visible face of the brand since its start.

That context is why the Couto post drew attention. A campaign centered on another model suggests the company may be widening who represents it, rather than relying only on its founder. Whether that signals a lasting change in strategy has not been confirmed by SYRN or Sweeney.

Why it matters

For a celebrity-founded brand, who appears in campaigns can shape how the business is seen. Featuring a named model can help a label present itself as a company with its own identity, not only an extension of its founder's public profile. At the same time, Sweeney's name and image have been a major draw for SYRN, so any shift in balance could affect how the brand is marketed.

TheRichest also noted in its piece that Sweeney has faced public criticism over a separate advertisement for the sports-prediction platform Novig, and reported that she lost more than 200,000 Instagram followers. That is a different matter from the SYRN campaign, and the outlet did not link the two.

What's next: SYRN has not said whether Couto will appear in more campaigns. Fans will likely watch the brand's Instagram to see whether it keeps featuring other models or returns its focus to Sweeney.

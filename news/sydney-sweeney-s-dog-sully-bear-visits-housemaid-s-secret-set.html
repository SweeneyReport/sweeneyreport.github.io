Sydney Sweeney's Dog Sully Bear Shows Up on Housemaid Set

Sydney Sweeney's German Shepherd, Sully Bear, reportedly dropped by the Jersey City set of The Housemaid's Secret, the sequel to her 2025 thriller.

By Sweeney Report · November 20, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's German Shepherd, Sully Bear, made a visit to the set of The Housemaid's Secret. ComingSoon reported that the actress was seen with her dog in Jersey City, New Jersey, on November 18, 2026, spending time with him during a break in filming. The sequel to The Housemaid is directed by Paul Feig and is scheduled to reach theaters on December 17, 2027.

What the set footage showed

According to ComingSoon, video from the location showed Sweeney sitting on the steps of a brick building while she hugged Sully Bear and took selfies with him. The outlet said photographs and videos from the set captured the two together during the pause in production, and that the clips drew attention to her pet.

The report did not say whether Sully Bear is a regular presence on the production or whether this was a one-time visit.

How Sully Bear became public

This was not the first time Sweeney shared her dog with fans. ComingSoon noted that she posted photos of him on Instagram in April 2025, announcing that a German Shepherd puppy had joined her household. In that post, as quoted by the outlet, she called him "Sully Bear" and wrote that in their first two weeks together he had become her "set best friend," along with her bowling partner, nap buddy, "mutual water enthusiast" and travel companion.

That description of him as a set companion makes the Jersey City appearance a natural continuation of how she introduced him to the public.

About the film

The Housemaid's Secret follows the hit 2025 movie The Housemaid, in which Sweeney also starred. ComingSoon described the project as a psychological thriller adapted from Freida McFadden's 2023 novel, continuing the story of Millie Calloway, the character Sweeney is playing again.

The outlet listed Kirsten Dunst as Wendy Garrick, Michele Morrone as Enzo, Paul Anthony Kelly as Douglas Garrick and Brittany Snow as Marybeth, among others in the cast. Feig is directing.

The set visit comes while the sequel is still in production, more than a year before its planned release, and it offers a rare, lighthearted glimpse behind the scenes of a thriller franchise that has been closely followed since the first film.

What's next

The Housemaid's Secret is set to open in theaters on December 17, 2027. Filming continues in the meantime, and neither the studio nor Sweeney has reportedly said anything about Sully Bear appearing in the film itself. Fans will have to wait for more set updates, and possibly more posts from Sweeney's Instagram, to see if her new set best friend returns.

Sydney Sweeney Could Wave the F1 Las Vegas Checkered Flag?

Yahoo Sports lists Sydney Sweeney among five celebrities who could wave the checkered flag at the 2026 F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix, though nothing is confirmed.

By Sweeney Report · November 18, 2026

Sydney Sweeney could be handed the checkered flag at Formula 1's Las Vegas Grand Prix, though nothing has been confirmed. Yahoo Sports, in a piece from Athlon Sports writer Faizan Mirza published on November 16, named the actress among five celebrities who could receive the honor at the 2026 race. The Grand Prix is scheduled for this Saturday, November 21.

Why her name is on the list

Sweeney is not new to the role. She waved the flag at the 2025 NASCAR Cup Series Championship in Phoenix, a track record that gives her a clear precedent in motorsport ceremonies. Yahoo Sports pointed to that appearance when including her among the possible picks for the F1 event.

The article is a speculative roundup rather than an announcement. Neither Formula 1 nor the Las Vegas Grand Prix has reportedly said who will wave the flag, and Sweeney's representatives have not been cited as commenting on the idea.

Who else is in the running

The headline names WNBA star Sophie Cunningham as the other leading candidate. According to the report, Cunningham waved the checkered flag at the race in Austin last month, which shows that F1 has recently turned to athletes and celebrities from outside the sport for the moment.

The Las Vegas race has its own recent history with the tradition. Last year, actress Catherine Zeta-Jones was the one who waved the flag at the finish. That makes the Las Vegas flag a high-profile spot that organizers have filled with well-known entertainment figures before.

What it would mean

If Sweeney were chosen, it would add a second major motorsport flag-waving appearance within roughly a year, moving from NASCAR's championship weekend to Formula 1. It would also keep her in front of sports audiences at a time when the press follows her closely beyond her film and television work. For now, though, her inclusion is only Yahoo Sports' suggestion, and the list names several other possibilities.

What's next

The answer will come on Saturday, November 21, when the Las Vegas Grand Prix finishes and the checkered flag comes out. Until an official announcement, Sweeney's involvement should be treated as a possibility rather than a plan.

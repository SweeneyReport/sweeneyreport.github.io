Sydney Sweeney: Housemaid's Secret Teaser Is Hiding in Theaters

The first teaser for The Housemaid's Secret is reportedly playing before Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping screenings, ComingSoon reported.

By Sweeney Report · November 20, 2026

The first teaser for The Housemaid's Secret, the sequel starring Sydney Sweeney, is reportedly playing in theaters ahead of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. ComingSoon.net reported that moviegoers who see the Hunger Games film can catch the preview before it is released more widely. The sequel is set to open on December 17, 2027.

How to see the teaser

According to ComingSoon, the only way to watch the teaser for now is to attend a screening of Sunrise on the Reaping. The outlet noted that the film, based on the Suzanne Collins book of the same name, runs 2 hours and 30 minutes and carries a PG-13 rating. It suggested that fans check local showtimes, and said tickets are available through Fandango.

Why the sequel is moving ahead

The first Housemaid film, released in 2025, reportedly earned more than $400 million worldwide, according to ComingSoon. The outlet said that result prompted Lionsgate to move forward with another installment based on Freida McFadden's book series.

Sweeney returns as Millie Calloway, and Michele Morrone is reportedly reprising his role as Enzo. ComingSoon reported that Kirsten Dunst, Paul Anthony Kelly, Brittany Snow and Stephen Kalyn have joined the cast. Dunst plays Wendy Garrick, Kelly plays her billionaire husband Douglas Garrick, Snow plays Marybeth, and Kalyn plays Brock.

The story and the production

Per ComingSoon, the plot follows Millie as she takes a new housekeeping job for a woman she is never allowed to see. A mystery behind a locked door then threatens to expose secrets darker than her own.

Paul Feig returns as director, with Rebecca Sonnenshine writing the screenplay. Sweeney also serves as an executive producer alongside McFadden, the outlet said. ComingSoon added that production has already begun and that Sweeney and Morrone were recently spotted filming together in Hoboken, New Jersey.

What's next: Lionsgate has not been quoted in the source on a wider online release of the teaser, so it is unclear when it will reach the public. Until then, the theatrical run of Sunrise on the Reaping appears to be the only place to see it, with the sequel itself due on December 17, 2027.

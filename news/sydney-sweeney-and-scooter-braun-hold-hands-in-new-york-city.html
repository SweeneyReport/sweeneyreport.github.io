Sydney Sweeney and Scooter Braun Step Out in New York

Sydney Sweeney and Scooter Braun were photographed hand in hand in Manhattan's Upper West Side after a day of work on The Housemaid's Secret.

By Sweeney Report · October 31, 2026

Sydney Sweeney and Scooter Braun were photographed holding hands in New York City on Thursday, October 29, 2026. Hello! reported that the couple stepped out in the Upper West Side. The Sun and the Irish Star also published photos of the outing.

A night out after a day on set

According to Hello!, the outing followed a busy stretch of work for the actress, who is currently filming The Housemaid's Secret. Hello! described the project as a prequel to The Housemaid, in which Sweeney starred. The Sun's coverage also tied the evening to a long day of work on the production.

Hello! said Sweeney had been pictured on set in New Jersey on Monday. The outlet noted that she appeared there with longer hair than in recent appearances. No plot details or release plans for the prequel were included in the coverage reviewed for this article.

The relationship in the press

Hello! referred to Braun as Sweeney's boyfriend, and the Irish Star used the same description in its headline. The photos were credited by Hello! to GC Images. The outlets focused on the pair being seen together in public, and none of the reports quoted either of them about the outing.

Sweeney, 29 according to Hello!, has also been in the headlines for her recent hair change. Hello! recalled that she told The Hollywood Reporter her long hair had become a "security blanket" and that she felt she was going to "meet a new version of myself" after cutting it into a bob. That comment came from an earlier interview and was not connected to this week's outing.

Work and private life

The coverage highlights how Sweeney has been splitting her time between a demanding shoot and her personal life. Hello! also pointed to her recent campaigns for her SYRN brand, which have drawn plenty of attention and debate in recent months. The reports did not suggest any change in her filming schedule.

Neither Sweeney nor Braun had publicly commented on the photos at the time of writing, based on the sources reviewed.

What's next

What's next: Sweeney is expected to keep filming The Housemaid's Secret, with more set photos likely to surface as production continues. Any official update on the film or on her relationship would likely come from her team or from the actress herself.

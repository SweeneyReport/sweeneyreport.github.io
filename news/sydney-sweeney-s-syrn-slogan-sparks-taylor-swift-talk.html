Sydney Sweeney's SYRN Slogan Has Swifties Talking

A SYRN product reading "but daddy I love him" led fans to link Sydney Sweeney to Taylor Swift, though neither star has commented.

By Sweeney Report · October 25, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's latest SYRN drop has fans debating whether it was a nod to Taylor Swift. In July, the actress shared the new collection on her Instagram Stories, and one item carried the phrase "but daddy I love him," which is also the title of a Taylor Swift song. The Hollywood Gossip reported that the promotion amounted to a swipe at the singer. Neither Sweeney nor Swift has publicly addressed the claim.

What Sweeney said

Sweeney did not point to any connection herself. She described the drop only as featuring "some of my lil sayings," presenting the slogans as part of the playful, phrase-driven style of her SYRN lingerie brand. She did not mention Swift or the song in her post, according to the details cited by The Hollywood Gossip.

Why fans linked it to Scooter Braun

The theory took off because of a reported personal connection. Scooter Braun once owned the master recordings of Swift's early albums, a dispute that became one of the most discussed feuds in the music industry. Some fans pointed out that Sweeney is reportedly dating Braun, and they read the slogan as a pointed reference to that history. That relationship has been reported by the press, not confirmed by either of them, and the link between the slogan and the feud is purely fan interpretation.

A jab or a coincidence?

There is no evidence beyond the timing and the wording that the phrase was aimed at Swift. Slogans are common in the fashion and lingerie space, and the phrase may simply have been chosen because it was catchy and widely recognized. The Hollywood Gossip framed the promotion as a dig, but that is the outlet's reading, not a statement from Sweeney or her brand. Representatives for both stars had not commented on any link, based on the available reporting.

The episode shows how closely SYRN's marketing is watched. Each release draws attention not only from shoppers but also from fans who comb through every product name and caption for hidden meaning, which can drive extra conversation, and sometimes controversy, around the brand.

What's next

For now, the story rests on speculation. Unless Sweeney, Swift or Braun speak up, the question of whether the slogan was a jab or a coincidence is likely to stay unresolved, while fans wait to see what SYRN releases next.

Sydney Sweeney Started Her Own Production Company: Fifty-Fifty

Sydney Sweeney started Fifty-Fifty Films in 2020, and the company has since backed projects including Anyone But You, Immaculate and Christy.

By Sweeney Report · November 2, 2026

Sydney Sweeney moved behind the camera in 2020 when she launched her own production company, Fifty-Fifty Films. Deadline reported that its first project was The Players Table, a series in which she would star and serve as an executive producer. She has since taken producing credits on Anyone But You, Immaculate and Christy.

How Fifty-Fifty Films started

The company was created in 2020, during the pandemic, when production on Euphoria was paused, according to Cheat Sheet. Its stated aim was to bring women-led stories to film and television. Sweeney told Variety that she wanted to bring her own voice to the stories she imagined.

Deadline reported that The Players Table was to adapt Jessica Goodman's novel They Wish They Were Us. The project involved the production company Crazyrose and Endeavor Content. Annabelle Attanasio was attached to write, direct and executive produce, and the singer Halsey was also attached as a producer. Press coverage at the time described the series as being developed for HBO Max. This article has not confirmed its current status.

The meaning of the name

Sweeney has explained the company name in interviews. In a conversation with Emmys, she said she likes to be "fifty-fifty" with all of her partners. She framed it as a belief that collaboration makes projects stronger.

Producing credits

Fifty-Fifty Films has been linked to a growing list of titles. The romantic comedy Anyone But You and the horror film Immaculate were both released under her producing banner. Christy, the boxing biopic, followed with Sweeney again credited as a producer.

On Immaculate, Sweeney told Variety in 2024 that producing was a way to create opportunities for herself. She said that as a woman she felt she had to build the projects she wanted to be part of. She added that she wanted to produce projects she did not appear in, so that other people could tell their stories. Coverage of the film also said the idea had been around for about ten years. Sweeney revived it with director Michael Mohan and writer Andrew Lobel once she wanted to move into producing.

Some sources disagree on the exact founding date of the company, but 2020 is the date most often cited.

What's next

With several credits behind her, Sweeney's company has become a central part of her career alongside her acting work. Any new Fifty-Fifty Films announcements are likely to be reported by the trades, and this article will not speculate on them.

Sydney Sweeney: Madame Web vs Anyone But You Box Office

Madame Web grossed about $100 million worldwide, less than half of Anyone But You's roughly $220 million, while Immaculate earned about $35 million on a $9 million budget.

By Sweeney Report · October 29, 2026

Three Sydney Sweeney films released within about four months of each other ended up with very different box office results. According to The Numbers, Madame Web grossed roughly $100 million worldwide against an $80 million production budget. That was less than half of the roughly $220 million that Anyone But You earned on a $25 million budget. The horror film Immaculate reportedly brought in about $35 million on a budget of around $9 million.

Madame Web: a costly miss

Sony's Spider-Man spin-off opened in February 2024. The Numbers lists about $43.8 million from North America and about $56.5 million from international markets. GamesRadar and other outlets reported that the film opened to about $25.8 million over its six-day debut. ComicBook.com and others noted that it was the first Marvel-branded release not to open at number one since 2015's Fantastic Four.

Critics were largely negative. Reports from the period put its Rotten Tomatoes critics' score in the low teens, with an audience score in the mid-50s. Some outlets reported that the budget was higher than the $80 million figure, but that claim was not confirmed elsewhere.

Anyone But You: the surprise hit

The romantic comedy was released in December 2023 and opened to only about $6 million in the United States, according to The Numbers. It then kept earning through word of mouth and a long theatrical run. Slashfilm reported that it passed $200 million worldwide, and The Numbers lists a total of about $219.8 million. That is roughly 8.8 times its production budget. Sweeney celebrated the early $100 million milestone with the message "Rom coms are back baby!", Yahoo Entertainment reported.

Immaculate: a record for Neon

The horror film arrived in March 2024. Reports said it opened to about $5.3 million domestically, the best opening weekend in the United States for distributor Neon. Fangoria later reported a worldwide total of about $35.3 million. Earlier snapshots, such as an April 2024 Wikipedia tally of $22.3 million, were lower. The difference appears to reflect when each figure was recorded, though no source says so directly. Figures from different databases vary slightly, so all of the totals above are approximate.

What's next

Taken together, the three releases show how differently her projects performed with audiences. A lower-budget romantic comedy and a small horror film both far outperformed their costs, while the large studio release did not. Fans can now compare the three performances for themselves, as each film is available to watch at home.

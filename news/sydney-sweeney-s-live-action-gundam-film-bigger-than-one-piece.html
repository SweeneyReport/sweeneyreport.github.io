Sydney Sweeney's Gundam Film: Is It Bigger Than One Piece?

IMDb called Sydney Sweeney's Netflix Gundam movie bigger than One Piece, but the claim is unverified and no release date is confirmed.

By Sweeney Report · November 19, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's upcoming Netflix project, a live-action Gundam film, is being compared to One Piece. The movie comes from Legendary Pictures, with Jim Mickle directing, and Sweeney and Noah Centineo lead the cast. Filming has reportedly wrapped. Netflix is set to distribute it. No release date has been officially confirmed, though reports point to 2027.

What we know about the project

The film adapts Gundam, the long-running anime franchise, as a live-action feature. Legendary Pictures is behind the production. Jim Mickle is directing. Sweeney and Centineo headline the cast, and Netflix is attached as the distributor.

Beyond those details, little has been confirmed publicly. Plot specifics, the characters the leads play and the full supporting cast were not part of the material reviewed for this article. We will update this report if the studio or Netflix shares more.

The One Piece comparison

The comparison took off after IMDb published a news item. Its headline described the Gundam adaptation as officially bigger than One Piece. The claim is unverified. The IMDb piece did not come with confirmed figures from Netflix or Legendary that would settle the question. It is best read as a headline-style comparison, not an established fact.

One Piece is also a hugely popular anime property, and Netflix has adapted it in live action. That makes it a natural point of comparison for any new anime-based project on the platform. Whether Gundam will draw a similar audience is something only viewership numbers can show, and the film has not been released.

Why it matters for Sweeney

The project would put Sweeney at the center of a major franchise adaptation on a global streaming service. Her pairing with Centineo is one of the main talking points around the film. Fans of the anime and of her other work have both been following it closely. How the adaptation is received by longtime Gundam viewers will likely shape much of the conversation once footage appears.

What's next

The next milestones to watch are an official release date from Netflix and a first look or trailer. Reports currently point to 2027, but nothing has been confirmed. Until then, the "bigger than One Piece" label remains an unverified claim. Would you watch Gundam over One Piece?

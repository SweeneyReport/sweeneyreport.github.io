Sydney Sweeney's Housemaid Set Clip: Morrone Steps In

Clips from the New Jersey shoot of The Housemaid sequel reportedly show Michele Morrone stepping between Sydney Sweeney and Cooper Hoffman's characters.

By Sweeney Report · November 21, 2026

Michele Morrone was seen stepping in to protect Sydney Sweeney's character during filming of the Housemaid sequel, according to set clips that ComingSoon.net highlighted. In the footage, Morrone positions himself between Sweeney and Cooper Hoffman's character. Sweeney reportedly responds by pulling goofy faces behind him, and the exchange ends with the two costars hugging.

What the set clips showed

ComingSoon.net reported that the pair filmed scenes on November 18 in Jersey City before moving on to Hoboken. The outlet said the sidewalk scenes were captured on camera by onlookers, and videos of the moment spread online. The clips appear to show a tense beat that turns playful once the cameras stop, which suggests the cast is getting along well during the shoot.

The outlet also reported that crews worked at an uptown brownstone, and that neighborhood notices reportedly said filming would continue through Thursday. Several cast members were seen wearing cowboy hats near a new street light prop. The sequel itself is set in a Manhattan penthouse, according to the report.

Production had reportedly been in Hoboken earlier, on October 14 and 15, near and inside a coffee shop called The Hive. ComingSoon.net said Sweeney posed for photos with locals there.

Who is Michele Morrone?

Morrone joined the Lionsgate project after working with director Paul Feig on A Simple Favor 2, ComingSoon.net reported. He costars alongside Sweeney, Amanda Seyfried and Brandon Sklenar. The film is produced by Hidden Pictures and Feigco Entertainment, and Rebecca Sonnenshine adapted the screenplay from Freida McFadden's novel.

The actor is best known for playing Massimo Torricelli in Netflix's 365 Days, a Sicilian Mafia family member who gives a woman 365 days to fall in love with him. The 2020 film led to two sequels, 365 Days: This Day and The Next 365 Days. ComingSoon.net also reportedly listed his other upcoming work, including Bunny-Man with James Franco and Bella Thorne, and Bobby Moresco's Maserati: The Brothers with Anthony Hopkins, Andy Garcia and Jessica Alba.

The story so far

In the first film's story, Sweeney plays Millie, a struggling young woman who takes a housemaid job with a wealthy couple, Nina (Seyfried) and Andrew (Sklenar). She soon finds that their secrets are more dangerous than her own, per ComingSoon.net's summary of the plot.

It is not clear from the clips what Morrone's character is doing in this scene or whose side he is on, and no plot details were confirmed by the studio in the source report.

What's next: Filming was reportedly set to continue in the New Jersey area through the end of the week. Lionsgate had not confirmed Morrone's role or a release date in the material reviewed, so viewers will have to wait for official details on whom Millie can really trust.

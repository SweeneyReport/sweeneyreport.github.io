Sydney Sweeney Was Told Not To Audition For Euphoria

Sydney Sweeney has said a casting director first thought she did not fit Cassie, but a self-taped audition with her mom led to the Euphoria role.

By Sweeney Report · October 24, 2026

Sydney Sweeney has said she was told not to bother auditioning for HBO's Euphoria because a casting director initially did not think she suited the role of Cassie. According to coverage of an interview with The Hollywood Reporter, her agent still obtained the audition materials, she recorded a tape herself, and she booked the part after that single audition. Sweeney later earned two Emmy nominations in 2022, one of them for playing Cassie.

How the audition came together

Outlets including Nylon and Stylist reported that the casting director did not see Sweeney as right for Cassie and discouraged her from coming in. The coverage did not name that casting director. Some outlets reportedly speculated about who it was, but that was not confirmed.

Her team did not drop the idea. Sweeney's agent reportedly had other clients up for the same role, which gave them access to the script and audition materials. Sweeney then put herself on tape, with her mom reading the other characters' lines, and sent it in. She has said that one audition was enough to get her the part.

No hard feelings

Sweeney has not criticized the casting director. As quoted in coverage of the interview, she said: "No hate to the casting director," and added that she loves her now. Outlets have framed the story as an example of persistence in an industry where first impressions can decide who gets seen.

From Cassie to Emmy nominations

Euphoria premiered on HBO in 2019, and Cassie became one of the show's best-known characters. In July 2022, Sweeney received her first Emmy nominations, according to reports from outlets including ET and CBS News. One was for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Drama Series for Euphoria. The other was for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie for her role as Olivia Mossbacher in HBO's The White Lotus. Trade and entertainment coverage noted that being nominated for two different roles in the same year was uncommon.

The audition story has resurfaced repeatedly in the years since. Fans often cite it when discussing how Sweeney went from a role she was reportedly told she was wrong for to a defining part of her early career.

What's next

Sweeney's career has since grown well beyond Euphoria, and the audition story is likely to keep coming up in her interviews. For now, the question for fans is which Cassie scene they think was her best.

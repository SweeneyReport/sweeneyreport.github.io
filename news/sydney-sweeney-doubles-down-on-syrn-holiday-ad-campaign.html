Sydney Sweeney Doubles Down on SYRN Holiday Ads After Backlash

Sydney Sweeney kept posting content from her SYRN holiday campaign, including Home Alone recreations, after online criticism of her Cindy Lou Who promotion.

By Sweeney Report · October 28, 2026

Sydney Sweeney reportedly kept promoting her SYRN holiday line this week, sharing more material from a campaign that drew criticism online. The 29-year-old actress posted additional images and clips, including recreations inspired by Home Alone, according to AOL's syndicated reports from The Express and The Mirror. Both outlets said she appeared to be brushing off the backlash.

How the campaign began

Last week, Sweeney released a Christmas-themed promotion for SYRN, her intimate wear brand, on Instagram. In that first post, she appeared as Cindy Lou Who, the young character from How the Grinch Stole Christmas. The campaign was created to promote SYRN's new holiday collection.

The reaction

The Express reported that the initial post drew complaints from some people online, who called the creative choice "disgusting" and unsuitable. The reports did not name a specific critic or give a count of complaints, so the scale of the reaction is unclear. The Mirror described the response as a wave of fierce online backlash.

Rather than removing the content or responding directly, Sweeney reportedly continued the rollout. The Mirror reported that SYRN released a new series of photos and clips from the holiday line, including the Home Alone-themed material. Neither outlet, as quoted in the AOL syndications, included a statement from Sweeney or SYRN addressing the criticism.

Why it matters for the brand

Controversy has often driven attention to marketing campaigns, and SYRN's holiday push is now the subject of wide coverage. Whether that attention translates into sales has not been reported, and no figures on the line's performance have been released. Supporters and critics may read the decision to keep posting differently: some may see it as confident branding, while others may see it as ignoring concerns raised by part of the audience.

What's next: It remains to be seen whether SYRN will release more holiday content or comment publicly on the reaction. Neither the brand nor Sweeney has announced further plans for the campaign.

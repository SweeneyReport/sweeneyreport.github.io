Sydney Sweeney on Amanda Seyfried: "Like a Sister to Me"

Sydney Sweeney paid tribute to co-star Amanda Seyfried on her birthday, and Seyfried replied that it is "easy to support" her.

By Sweeney Report · November 15, 2026

Sydney Sweeney said her The Housemaid co-star Amanda Seyfried had become "like a sister" to her. She wrote it in an Instagram tribute on Seyfried's birthday, December 3, 2025. Paul Feig's thriller reached theaters on December 19, 2025. Seyfried answered in interviews that supporting Sweeney comes naturally to her.

The birthday tribute

Sweeney marked Seyfried's birthday on December 3 with an Instagram post. According to People and Parade, she wrote, "You've become like a sister to me." Parade also reported that she said she loved watching Seyfried go wild on set, a nod to the film's intense, chaotic scenes.

People reported that Sweeney keeps a Polaroid of the two of them from the set tucked into her phone case. It also reported that she told Seyfried how much the year had changed her life.

Seyfried's response

In an exclusive People interview published in early December 2025, Seyfried said it is "easy to support" Sweeney. She told her, "You're thoughtful as hell, and it's been such a dream to do this with you," according to the outlet. People reported that Seyfried, 40, said the two related over shared experiences. Sweeney was 28 at the time.

Feig told People he was not prepared for the level of chemistry between the two actors.

The slap that ended in laughter

Seyfried also reportedly said she hesitated when it came time to slap Sweeney on camera. Pakistan's Express Tribune reported that she was unsure how to deliver the slap, and the moment reportedly ended in laughs on set. The exact wording of that exchange could not be confirmed in a primary interview, so the details differ between reports.

About the film

The Housemaid is based on the bestselling novel. People described Sweeney as playing Millie, a live-in maid, and Seyfried as Nina, the woman who employs her. Brandon Sklenar and Michele Morrone also star. Seyfried told People that smashing things in character was "so cathartic."

Sweeney also described the friendship in a Variety interview, where she said the two had become sisters and called Seyfried "the bestest friend ever," according to a summary of that interview.

What's next

Neither actor has announced a new project together, so any reunion remains speculation for now. Given the chemistry Feig described and the warmth in both actors' comments, fans have been asking whether the two will team up again.

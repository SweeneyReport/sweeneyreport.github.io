Sydney Sweeney's Barbarella Remake: Is It Still Happening?

Deadline reported in June 2026 that Sydney Sweeney's Barbarella remake is still active at Sony, now under her new company Honey Trap.

By Sweeney Report · November 17, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's Barbarella remake is still alive. Deadline reported in June 2026 that the project is still moving forward at Sony Pictures. Sweeney is attached to star and is also an executive producer. The update followed a long quiet stretch that had led some fans to assume the film had been shelved. No release date has been announced.

How the project began

Deadline first reported in October 2022 that Sweeney was attached to star in a new version of Barbarella for Sony. The original 1968 film starred Jane Fonda and was based on Jean-Claude Forest's comics. Variety noted that it was not a box office hit at the time but later became a cult favorite.

In May 2024, Variety reported that Edgar Wright was in talks to direct the new film. The outlet also reported that Jane Goldman and Honey Ross were in talks to write the screenplay. Variety noted that Sweeney had previously worked with Sony on Anyone but You and Madame Web. It also reported that she described Madame Web as a strategic business decision that helped her secure this project.

Why the 2026 update matters

According to Deadline, Sweeney is now developing the film through Honey Trap, her new production company. Honey Trap has a first-look deal with Sony, and Sweeney is reportedly already working on Barbarella under that arrangement. The report suggests the remake has become part of her wider push into producing, alongside her acting work.

Little else has been confirmed publicly. Dark Horizons, summarizing the Deadline report, said the article did not confirm whether Wright is still attached to direct. His last reported involvement dates to 2024, so his current status remains unclear. The report also gave no production start date, casting details or release window.

Mixed history of the remake

The remake has drawn attention since it was announced. Variety reported that Fonda voiced some hesitation about a reboot in 2023. Sony has not announced a timeline, and the long gap between the 2024 reports and the June 2026 update explains why many fans had doubted the project.

What's next

Sony has not announced a release date, a confirmed director or a shooting schedule. Any further news will most likely come through trade outlets such as Deadline and Variety, so fans will have to wait for official confirmation of the creative team and the timeline.

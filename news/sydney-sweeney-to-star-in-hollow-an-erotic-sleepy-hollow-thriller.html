Sydney Sweeney's Sleepy Hollow Is Now an Erotic Thriller

Sydney Sweeney is reportedly set to play Katrina Van Tassel in Hollow, Lindsey Anderson Beer's gothic erotic thriller based on her own debut novel.

By Sweeney Report · November 8, 2026

Sydney Sweeney is reportedly attached to star in Hollow, a new screen take on the Sleepy Hollow legend that is being described as an erotic thriller. According to Numéro Magazine, she would play Katrina Van Tassel, while writer and director Lindsey Anderson Beer, known for Sierra Burgess Is a Loser, is set to direct. The film adapts Beer's own debut novel, which is due to be published in autumn 2027.

A different Katrina

Numéro cited Deadline in reporting that the project would present Katrina not as a romantic prize but as the central figure of a dangerous mystery and a supernatural love triangle. In this version, Numéro wrote, Katrina leads the investigation rather than serving as the object of other characters' ambitions.

Deadline's description, as relayed by Numéro, said the film would combine gothic atmosphere, psychological intrigue and erotic thriller elements.

The casting of Ichabod Crane

IMDb News reported that Nicholas Galitzine, who appeared in The Idea of You, reportedly joined the project. Numéro was more cautious, saying he could take on the role of Ichabod Crane. The casting should therefore be treated as unconfirmed for now.

Background: a story with many lives

The source is Washington Irving's short story The Legend of Sleepy Hollow, published in 1820. It has been adapted many times, according to Numéro, including a 1949 Disney animated version, a 1980 television film with Jeff Goldblum, and the Fox series Sleepy Hollow, which ran from 2013 to 2017.

The best-known screen version is probably Tim Burton's 2000 film, which Numéro noted won the Oscar for Best Art Direction. In it, Christina Ricci played Katrina and Johnny Depp played Ichabod Crane. Numéro observed that Burton's film already leaned into a mystical side that the new project may push further.

What's next

No release date, studio or production start has been confirmed in the sources reviewed. The book is expected to come out in autumn 2027, and further casting and production details will likely follow. Until then, the leads remain reported rather than officially announced.

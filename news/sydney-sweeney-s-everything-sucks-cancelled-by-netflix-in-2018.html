Sydney Sweeney's Netflix Show Cancelled After One Season

Netflix ended Everything Sucks!, the 1990s teen comedy featuring Sydney Sweeney, in April 2018 after one ten-episode season, despite fan and cast support.

By Sweeney Report · November 16, 2026

Netflix cancelled Everything Sucks!, the nineties-set teen comedy that featured Sydney Sweeney, in April 2018, less than two months after it premiered. The series debuted on February 16, 2018, with a single season of ten half-hour episodes. Sweeney played Emaline Addario, a member of the school drama club. Years later, the show still has a loyal following online.

The show and Sweeney's role

Everything Sucks! was set in 1996 at the fictional Boring High School in Boring, Oregon. It followed students in the A/V Club and the Drama Club as their paths crossed. According to Wikipedia, Emaline was a junior and the ex-girlfriend of the character Oliver, and she was written as a dramatic personality. Peyton Kennedy also starred in the series, and the show was co-created by Ben York Jones and Michael Mohan.

Reviews and the cancellation

Critics were generally positive. Wikipedia lists a 72% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes for the season, based on 47 reviews, and a Metacritic score of 62 out of 100. Reviewers praised the coming-out storyline and the performances of Peyton Kennedy and Jahi Di'Allo Winston. Some criticized the heavy use of genre tropes and the thinly developed supporting characters.

Netflix confirmed the cancellation on April 6, 2018, according to Wikipedia. Outlets covering the news at the time reported that the season ended on a cliffhanger and that the creators had already started mapping out a possible second season. Executive producer Jeff Pinkner said the team was grateful to Netflix but "very disheartened" not to continue telling these stories, according to coverage of the announcement.

Cast and fan reaction

Several cast and crew members reportedly posted farewell messages on social media and encouraged fans to campaign for a renewal. Some fans also voiced disappointment toward Netflix. A fan effort to save the show followed, but no second season was ever ordered, and no other platform picked the series up.

The show has since gained a reputation as a cult favorite among viewers who discovered it after its cancellation. This is a fan-driven reputation rather than an official designation.

What's next

There has been no announcement of a revival of Everything Sucks!. Sweeney has gone on to much larger projects, but fans continue to ask which of her early roles deserves a second chance, and this one regularly comes up.

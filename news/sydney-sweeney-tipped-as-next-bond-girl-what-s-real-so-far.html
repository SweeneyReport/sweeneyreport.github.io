Sydney Sweeney Tipped as the Next Bond Girl? What's Real

Sydney Sweeney's name has surfaced in Bond casting talk, but no female lead has been announced and the speculation remains unconfirmed.

By Sweeney Report · November 17, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's name has come up in speculation about the next Bond girl, but nothing has been confirmed. The Daily Mail and Marca both reported that the actress is among the stars mentioned as possible contenders, alongside Kendall Jenner and Maya Jama. Amazon MGM has not officially announced a female lead for the franchise.

Where the speculation started

The talk arrived as attention on the next 007 grew. Marca's headline described Sweeney, Jenner and Jama as top contenders for the role opposite Robert Pattinson, who is reportedly Amazon's frontrunner to play the spy. The Daily Mail published its own list of names under a headline that treated Pattinson's casting as settled.

That framing goes further than the evidence in hand. Amazon MGM has not officially announced its Bond, and the Hollywood Reporter reported that Pattinson screen-tested in London in late October. A screen test is a step in the process, not a confirmed deal.

What is and is not known

Both outlets presented the female names as possibilities. Neither article, as summarized in the reporting reviewed here, pointed to an offer, an audition or a statement from Sweeney's team. No studio representative has commented on any of the actresses named.

The lists read more like a media wish list than a casting report. Entertainment outlets often put forward popular names when a major franchise role is in play, and those names regularly never turn out to be involved.

Why the name keeps coming up

Sweeney has been a frequent subject of casting talk since her rise on television and in film, and she also runs her own business ventures. Her inclusion in the lists suggests she is seen as a high-profile name, though that is the outlets' framing and not an indication of any actual discussions.

What's next: For now, the story is unresolved. Viewers should watch for an official announcement from Amazon MGM about the lead and the wider cast. Until then, Sweeney's link to a Bond film is speculation only, and any claim that she has been cast should be treated as unverified.

Sydney Sweeney's Honey Trap Dark Comedy Lands Sales Agent

Littermates, a dark comedy produced by Sydney Sweeney's Honey Trap, has signed with Dawson for sales at the American Film Market, Screen Daily reported.

By Sweeney Report · November 13, 2026

Littermates, a dark comedy produced by Sydney Sweeney's company Honey Trap, has found a sales agent, Screen Daily reported. Dawson added the film to its American Film Market slate, while CAA Media Finance is jointly handling worldwide sales, according to the outlet.

What the film is about

Screen Daily reported that the story follows a pair of injured siblings who arrive at a remote cottage in England while an unexplained war unfolds around them. The outlet described the project as a dark comedy.

Kaylee McGregor stars in the film. According to Screen Daily, she also produces for Honey Trap, meaning she holds a role both in front of the camera and behind the scenes on the project.

Festival track record

Littermates already has some festival credentials, according to Screen Daily. The outlet reported that the film won a jury prize at the Manchester Film Festival and that it screened at Fantastic Fest. A sales deal at the American Film Market is often the step that lets a festival title reach buyers in different territories, though no distribution deals were mentioned in the report.

Honey Trap's growing slate

Honey Trap is Sweeney's production company, and Littermates is presented by Screen Daily as one of its productions. The report places the film among the projects the company has backed as a producer, rather than as a vehicle for Sweeney herself. Screen Daily did not say that Sweeney appears in the film, and nothing in the report suggested that she does.

The pairing of Dawson and CAA Media Finance on worldwide sales signals that the film is being positioned for an international audience at the market. Screen Daily did not detail pricing, buyers or any territories already sold.

What's next

With the American Film Market underway, buyers will now be able to look at Littermates alongside the rest of Dawson's slate. No release date or platform has been announced, according to the information available in Screen Daily's report. Any distribution agreements would likely be announced after the market begins to produce deals.

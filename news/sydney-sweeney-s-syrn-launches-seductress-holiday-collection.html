Sydney Sweeney's SYRN Kicks Off Seductress Season

Sydney Sweeney's SYRN opened its Seductress holiday shop on Thursday, after a reportedly record quarter for the lingerie brand.

By Sweeney Report · November 20, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's lingerie brand SYRN launched its "Seductress" holiday collection on Thursday. The brand announced the drop on its Instagram account with a winter wonderland photo shoot. The caption read, "The first flurry of Seductress season." Page Six reported that the launch comes as the company is reportedly enjoying its strongest quarter since it started.

How the holiday line has unfolded

The Seductress shop is one chapter of a larger seasonal rollout. Page Six reported that SYRN teased its holiday offering last month with a Christmas movie-themed ad campaign. Sweeney appeared in four themed looks inspired by Home Alone, How the Grinch Stole Christmas, Corpse Bride and White Christmas. According to Page Six, the White Christmas theme was tied to the Seductress line.

The collection is organized by theme. Page Six reported that the "Comfy" chapter came first, with its Home Alone feature at the end of October. Seductress follows, and "Playful" and "Romantic" are still to come.

A reportedly record quarter

Page Six also reported that a source shared exclusive figures on the brand's performance. According to that source, SYRN's third-quarter sales were double those of the second quarter. The source also said sales were more than three times the total for June. SYRN has not published these numbers, so they remain unconfirmed. Page Six noted that some people had doubted the Euphoria star's move into fashion.

Reaction and the Bond chatter

ComingSoon.net reported that the Instagram post drew a flood of supportive comments from fans. One commenter called her a "Bond girl," which touched on long-running speculation about the next 007 films. ComingSoon.net noted that Sweeney has been mentioned by fans as a possible Bond girl for more than a year. It also said she addressed the talk in an earlier Variety interview. She said she did not know all the rumors but was a big fan of the franchise. Asked whether she would take a role, she reportedly answered that it would depend on the script. ComingSoon.net added that Sweeney reportedly has a close working relationship with Amazon founder Jeff Bezos. Amazon owns MGM, which holds creative control over Bond. The outlet said Bezos reportedly helped back SYRN as well. Neither Sweeney nor Amazon has confirmed a Bond role.

What's next: The "Playful" and "Romantic" chapters are still due, and they will show whether the brand can sustain the sales momentum described by Page Six's source. Neither SYRN nor Sweeney has confirmed release dates for those themes.

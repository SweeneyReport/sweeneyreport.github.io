Sydney Sweeney: Her Euphoria Co-Star Is Now Tipped For Bond

Fans are backing Euphoria's Alexa Demie as the next Bond girl, while Sydney Sweeney has reportedly been in the running, according to the Daily Star.

By Sweeney Report · November 21, 2026

Fans have started a campaign to see Alexa Demie, who played Maddy Perez in Euphoria, cast as the next Bond girl. The Daily Star reported that her former co-star Sydney Sweeney has reportedly also been in the running for the role, allegedly with the backing of Amazon boss Jeff Bezos. Online talk of a feud between the two actresses remains unconfirmed.

How the fan push began

According to the Daily Star, Page Six shared a fan's proposal that Demie should join the next 007 film, and the idea gained traction online. The official Euphoria account replied with the words "Full support." Other fans wrote, "Exactly give the people what they want," and asked, "Where do we start a petition," the outlet said.

The Daily Star added that several other names have been tipped for the part, including Margaret Qualley, Florence Pugh, Hunter Schafer, Zendaya, Maya Jama and Jodie Comer. It also noted that Suki Waterhouse, the partner of Robert Pattinson, has been mentioned.

Sweeney and the feud rumours

The Daily Star reported that Sweeney was reportedly in the running and that she was said to have the support of Jeff Bezos, whose company has taken control of the Bond franchise. None of this has been officially confirmed.

The outlet also said unconfirmed rumours of tension between Sweeney and Demie have circulated online. Sweeney has dismissed reports of a Euphoria cast feud, and the two were seen hugging at the Season 3 premiere in Los Angeles in April, according to the Daily Star.

Asked about Bond rumours last year, Sweeney said, as quoted by the Daily Star: "Depends on the script. I think I'd have more fun as James Bond."

The next Bond film

Pattinson is set to play Bond, succeeding Daniel Craig, who first took the role in Casino Royale in 2006 and left after No Time To Die in 2021. The Daily Star said Denis Villeneuve will direct, with Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing.

No leading lady has been announced, and nothing in the reports confirms that either Sweeney or Demie has been approached or has an offer.

What's next: Casting for the film has not been officially confirmed, so the names circulating remain speculation for now. Any announcement from the producers would settle the question of who joins Pattinson on screen.

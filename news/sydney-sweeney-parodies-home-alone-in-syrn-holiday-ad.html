Sydney Sweeney Parodies Home Alone in New Syrn Holiday Campaign

Sydney Sweeney's Syrn brand released a Home Alone parody video to launch its Hibernation Nation holiday collection of knitted sets.

By Sweeney Report · November 1, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's lingerie and loungewear brand Syrn released a holiday video parodying the Christmas classic Home Alone. The clip launches the label's "Hibernation Nation" collection of cozy knitted sets, according to Kursiv Media. The brand shared the campaign on its Instagram account, and Page Six and NEWS.am STYLE also covered it.

What the video shows

Kursiv Media reported that the video recreates several moments from the 1990 film. In the best-known one, Sweeney presses her hands to her cheeks and lets out a dramatic scream. That is the pose Macaulay Culkin made famous as Kevin McCallister. NEWS.am STYLE reported that this scene also involved shaving cream and a razor, as in the movie.

Kursiv Media said another scene shows Sweeney happily digging into a large dessert topped with whipped cream. It echoes a moment in which Kevin enjoys a treat on his own. In a third sequence, she holds a small dog and nervously watches a curtain while a mysterious figure appears from behind it. NEWS.am STYLE described the dog as a white poodle and said other scenes include a knock at the door and a TV-watching moment with ice cream.

A pattern of themed campaigns

NEWS.am STYLE noted that the video continues a run of film-inspired Syrn promotions. It said Sweeney previously drew on Cindy Lou Who from How the Grinch Stole Christmas and on characters from The Nightmare Before Christmas. Parodies of familiar movies appear to be a recurring way for the brand to mark the holidays.

Kursiv Media reported that the festive video has delighted fans. It did not give specific figures for views or sales, and none are included here.

The brand behind it

Kursiv Media reported that Sweeney launched Syrn earlier this year. It said she has stressed that she designs pieces she wears herself and wants to make comfortable, stylish clothing for women of all body types. The Hibernation Nation line is the brand's seasonal offering, built around knitted sets.

What's next

Syrn has not announced its next campaign, so it is unclear whether another film parody is coming. The holiday season is likely to bring more releases from the brand, and the Sweeney Report will follow any new drops or collaborations as they are announced.

Sydney Sweeney's Role Before Euphoria You Might Have Missed

Before Cassie Howard in Euphoria, Sydney Sweeney played Eden, a young wife in season two of Hulu's The Handmaid's Tale.

By Sweeney Report · November 16, 2026

Years before her two Emmy nominations, Sydney Sweeney appeared in Hulu's dystopian drama The Handmaid's Tale. In season two, which premiered in 2018, she played Eden, a very young wife married to Nick Blaine. The part was recurring, and it came shortly before she took on Cassie Howard in HBO's Euphoria.

Who was Eden?

According to Wikipedia's filmography, Sweeney played Eden Spencer in seven episodes of season two. Eden was introduced in the fifth episode, "Seeds." Her marriage to Nick, played by Max Minghella, was a central part of her storyline. Nick's own marriage was also the subject of a Hollywood Reporter explainer on the episode.

Sweeney later described the character as a true believer in Gilead, the regime at the center of the series. As quoted by BuzzFeed News, she said Eden grew up inside it and did not know anything else. Some websites wrongly list Sweeney in other seasons of the show. Her only appearance was in season two.

A busy stretch

The Hulu role was not her only work in 2018. That year she also appeared in the Netflix series Everything Sucks!, where she played Emaline Addario across ten episodes, according to Wikipedia.

Her next big television step was Euphoria. She played Cassie Howard from 2019, and the series has run through 2026 according to Wikipedia. The role became one of her best-known parts.

Awards recognition

At the 74th Primetime Emmy Awards in 2022, Sweeney received two nominations. One was for supporting actress in a drama series for Euphoria. The other was for supporting actress in a limited or anthology series or movie for The White Lotus, in which she played Olivia Mossbacher in 2021.

Viewed in that order, Eden looks like an early step. It was a small but memorable part in a prestige series, and it came before the roles that brought her wider attention.

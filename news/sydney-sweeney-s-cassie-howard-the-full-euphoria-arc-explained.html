Sydney Sweeney's Cassie Howard: The Full Euphoria Arc

From a 2019 debut to a 2022 Emmy nomination and a series finale on May 31, 2026, here is how Sydney Sweeney's Cassie Howard story played out.

By Sweeney Report · November 16, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's Cassie Howard became one of the defining characters of HBO's Euphoria, and the role earned the actress an Emmy nomination in 2022. The series ended with its third season, which aired its finale on May 31, 2026. Creator Sam Levinson said on The New York Times' Popcast that there would be no fourth season, and TheWrap reported that he called Rue's fate "the honest ending."

Where Cassie began

Sweeney was cast in the role in June 2019, according to her Wikipedia biography, and Euphoria debuted that year. Cassie was introduced as Lexi's older sister and Maddy's best friend. Her first-season storyline followed a teenager whose life became more complicated after a pregnancy involving McKay.

Season two and the Emmy nomination

The second season, released in 2022, moved Cassie into a relationship with Nate, who was Maddy's ex. That betrayal of her close friend became the engine of her story that year. At the 74th Primetime Emmy Awards, Sweeney was nominated for outstanding supporting actress in a drama series for the role. She was also nominated that year for The White Lotus in the limited or anthology supporting actress category.

Season three and the ending

Sweeney returned as Cassie for the third season, which premiered on April 12, 2026, according to Wikipedia. Sources differ on some details of the run, and some outlets gave a different premiere date. NBC stations reported that the season divided viewers, with some of the discussion centered on Cassie's storylines.

TheWrap reported that the finale ended with the death of Zendaya's Rue after she took painkillers laced with fentanyl, and that Nate, played by Jacob Elordi, was killed off in the penultimate episode. HBO had described the finale as a season finale rather than a series finale, which prompted speculation about a return. Levinson's Popcast comments then made clear that the show was finished. Zendaya had also said earlier that she believed this was the end, TheWrap noted.

TheWrap also pointed out that Cassie was among four original characters, alongside Jules, Maddy and Lexi, who could in theory have carried the story forward. With the series over, that possibility is closed.

What's next

With Euphoria concluded, Sweeney's career continues beyond Cassie. Fans are still debating whether the character received the ending she deserved, and Levinson's decision means the story stays as it is.

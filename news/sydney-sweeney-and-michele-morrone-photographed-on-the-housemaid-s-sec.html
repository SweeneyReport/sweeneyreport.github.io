Sydney Sweeney Embraces Michele Morrone On Set: What's Going On?

TMZ published set photos of Sydney Sweeney embracing Michele Morrone in New York City and said the moment was a scene for The Housemaid's Secret.

By Sweeney Report · November 7, 2026

Sydney Sweeney and Michele Morrone were photographed in an embrace on the New York City set of The Housemaid's Secret, according to TMZ. The outlet reported that the photos were taken earlier this week and that the moment was part of the production, not a private encounter.

What the photos show

TMZ published a set of images showing the two actors close together during filming. The outlet framed the pictures with a playful tone, telling readers that the moment might raise eyebrows but that it was "just acting." In its report, TMZ described the embrace as a scene being shot for the movie.

The publication also turned the gallery into a small game. It invited readers to find three differences between two of the photos, and said the answers would be revealed in the gallery. TMZ did not report any plot details about the scene itself.

Why set photos matter

Photos taken during production often give audiences an early sense of a film's tone, locations and character dynamics. In this case, the images offer a first look at how Sweeney and Morrone might appear together in The Housemaid's Secret, though a single frame from a shoot says little about how a scene will be used in the finished film.

Fans have followed the project closely, and any glimpse of the cast at work tends to draw attention online. Neither Sweeney nor Morrone had publicly commented on the photos in the material reviewed for this article, and no studio statement about the scene was included in TMZ's report.

The takeaway

The key point from TMZ's coverage is that the embrace was described as filmed work, not as anything off-camera. Reports about set photos can sometimes be read out of context, so the outlet's clarification is the main factual detail available at this stage.

What's next: More set photos or official first-look material may appear as filming continues, but no release details were included in TMZ's report. Until the studio shares more, the images remain a brief preview of the pairing.

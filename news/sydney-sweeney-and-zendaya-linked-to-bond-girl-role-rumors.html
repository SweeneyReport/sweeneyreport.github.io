Sydney Sweeney vs Zendaya: The Bond Girl Buzz Explained

Star reportedly says Zendaya has the edge over Sydney Sweeney for a Bond girl part opposite Robert Pattinson, but nothing is confirmed.

By Sweeney Report · November 21, 2026

A Bond girl rivalry is reportedly taking shape between Zendaya and Sydney Sweeney, according to Star, which was republished by Yahoo Entertainment on November 20. The talk follows the announcement that Robert Pattinson will be the next James Bond. No female lead has been confirmed, and the claims rest on an unnamed source.

The 007 announcement

The official James Bond Instagram account announced on Monday, November 16, that Pattinson, 40, would take over the role of 007 from Daniel Craig. Yahoo's report noted that Amazon MGM Studios has tapped Denis Villeneuve to direct the film.

That news set off speculation about who might play opposite Pattinson, and Sweeney's name has come up in that conversation. Neither she nor her representatives were quoted as commenting on the matter in the material reviewed.

What the source claimed

Star quoted an unnamed source who said many people believe Zendaya has the advantage because she and Pattinson have a proven track record together. The source pointed to their film The Drama, which the source said performed very well at the box office, and said the pair had recently worked together again on Dune: Part Three.

The same source reportedly suggested that Villeneuve's involvement as director could help Zendaya's case, given his connection to the Dune franchise. The headline also claimed there was already "plenty of sniping going on" and that Zendaya would hate losing out, though those characterizations come solely from Star's anonymous source.

Why caution is needed

Nothing in the report indicates that either actress has been approached, auditioned or offered a part. Amazon MGM Studios has not announced any casting for a female lead, and the story is built on a single tabloid source. Reports of this kind about rivalries between actresses often prove to be speculation, so the claims should be treated as unverified.

What's next

What's next: Casting for Pattinson's first Bond film has not been officially revealed, so any lead actress will have to be confirmed by Amazon MGM Studios. Until then, the Zendaya and Sweeney talk remains unconfirmed buzz. We will update this story if the studio or either actress addresses it.

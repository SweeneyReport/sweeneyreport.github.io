Sydney Sweeney's Madame Web Hits HBO Max November 14

Madame Web, with Sydney Sweeney as Julia Cornwall, arrives on HBO Max on November 14 after a box office run that Screen Rant says fell short.

By Sweeney Report · October 25, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's superhero film Madame Web is set to arrive on HBO Max on November 14. Sweeney plays Julia Cornwall, one of the future Spider-Women, in a movie headlined by Dakota Johnson as Cassandra Webb and directed by S.J. Clarkson. IMDb flagged the streaming date in a recent headline, describing the film as a sci-fi thriller and counting down to its release roughly one month ahead.

The film and its cast

In Madame Web, Johnson stars as Cassandra Webb, the central character of the story. Sweeney appears as Julia Cornwall, one of the younger characters presented as a future Spider-Woman. Clarkson directed the project, which sits in the Spider-Man-adjacent comic book genre.

The streaming debut gives viewers who skipped the film in theaters another way to watch it. It also gives Sweeney's fans a chance to see an early entry in her run of big-studio roles.

The box office picture

The movie's commercial results have been a recurring talking point. Screen Rant reported that Madame Web had a budget of about 80 million dollars. According to the outlet, it earned only around 100 million dollars worldwide. Screen Rant said that total fell short of the film's estimated break-even point.

That gap between cost and earnings is a common yardstick for judging whether a big-budget film succeeded. Screen Rant's assessment is that this one did not clear the bar. The figures are the outlet's reported numbers, and break-even estimates for major studio releases vary because marketing and distribution costs are not always made public.

Sequel prospects

Screen Rant also reported that a sequel looks unlikely. The outlet tied that outlook to the film's modest performance. No studio announcement about a follow-up was cited in the material reviewed for this article, so the future of the story remains unconfirmed.

Streaming often gives films a second life, since viewers can sample them at home without the cost of a ticket. Whether that happens here will depend on how audiences respond once the film is available on HBO Max.

What's next

The next date to watch is November 14, when Madame Web becomes available on HBO Max. Streaming viewership could shape how the film is remembered, even if a sequel does not materialize. For Sweeney, the release adds visibility to a part of her filmography that drew mixed attention in theaters. Fans will be watching to see whether the platform brings the movie a new audience.

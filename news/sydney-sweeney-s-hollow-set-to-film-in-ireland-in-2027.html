Sydney Sweeney's Sleepy Hollow Thriller Is Filming In Ireland

Co-star Belmont Cameli told Deadline that the Sleepy Hollow retelling with Sydney Sweeney will shoot in Ireland in spring 2027.

By Sweeney Report · October 26, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's next thriller, Hollow, is set to begin filming in Ireland in spring 2027. IFTN reported that co-star Belmont Cameli revealed the plan in an interview with Deadline, and said he has already started horse-riding training. The film reimagines Washington Irving's The Legend of Sleepy Hollow from a new angle, with Sweeney playing Katrina Van Tassel.

A new take on a classic story

According to IFTN, the project shifts the focus of the familiar tale onto Katrina, placing her at the centre of a supernatural mystery and a love triangle. Cameli plays Brom Bones, the rival of schoolteacher Ichabod Crane. IFTN described the film as a blend of gothic horror, psychological intrigue and elements of an erotic thriller.

Cameli told Deadline, as quoted by IFTN: "In the spring, I'm going to film Hollow in Ireland with Sydney Sweeney."

Who is behind it

Lindsey Anderson Beer writes and directs, adapting her own forthcoming debut novel. IFTN reported that the book is due from Putnam in autumn 2027. Beer previously directed Pet Sematary: Bloodlines.

LuckyChap is producing alongside Beer, through her Lab Brew banner, and Sweeney, through her Honey Trap company. The film is in development at Sony Pictures. Cameli's recent credits include Until Dawn, The Alto Knights and the Prime Video college hockey drama Off Campus, according to IFTN.

What is still unknown

Details remain thin. IFTN noted that specific Irish filming locations, an Irish production partner and a release date have not yet been announced. The outlet did not report a start date beyond "spring."

What's next: Watch for announcements on the Irish production partner, filming locations and a release date. The novel's publication in autumn 2027 should also shed more light on Katrina's version of the story.

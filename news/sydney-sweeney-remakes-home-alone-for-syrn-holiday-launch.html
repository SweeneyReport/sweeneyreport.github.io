Sydney Sweeney Remakes Home Alone In New ET Video

Entertainment Tonight published a video in which Sydney Sweeney plays Kevin McCallister in a Home Alone remake promoting her SYRN holiday launch.

By Sweeney Report · October 29, 2026

Sydney Sweeney reportedly turned a Christmas classic into a marketing campaign. Entertainment Tonight published a new video on its YouTube channel in which she plays Kevin McCallister, the lead character of Home Alone, in a remake. According to the video's description, the project was made to promote the holiday launch of her lingerie brand, SYRN.

What we know about the video

Entertainment Tonight's description said Sweeney "channels" the iconic character in a new take on the family comedy. It also said the remake was created for the holiday launch of her lingerie line. That is the extent of the information available at the moment.

The description and title are the only material we have to go on. We have not seen the scenes, so we are not going to describe what happens in them. We also do not know who directed the remake, how long it runs, or whether other people appear in it. Entertainment Tonight has not provided those details in the text we reviewed.

Why it matters for SYRN

The video is an example of a brand campaign borrowing a well-known holiday movie. Home Alone is closely tied to Christmas, which makes it a natural fit for a seasonal product launch. Sweeney is both the face of SYRN and the person behind the business, so a creative piece like this puts her at the center of the brand's marketing as well as her acting career.

Entertainment Tonight's decision to publish the video on its own channel also gives the campaign exposure beyond SYRN's own social media. We do not have any numbers on views, sales or the size of the launch, and none were reported in the description.

Reaction so far

No reactions from Sweeney, her team, the makers of the original film or other celebrities appeared in the material we reviewed. Any early response from viewers would be unverified at this stage, so we are not characterizing the public's reaction.

What's next

The full video is available on the Entertainment Tonight YouTube channel. Further details about the SYRN holiday collection and the campaign behind the remake should become clearer as the launch moves forward, and we will update this story if Sweeney, SYRN or Entertainment Tonight share more information.

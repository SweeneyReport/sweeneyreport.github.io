Sydney Sweeney's Novig Ad Fueled an 80% Growth Surge

Sydney Sweeney shared a Fortune report showing her part-owned prediction market Novig grew 80% in 30 days after the "Just Sports" campaign.

By Sweeney Report · November 17, 2026

Sydney Sweeney drew attention to the rapid growth of Novig, the sports prediction market she part owns. She shared a Fortune report on her Instagram Stories, and ComingSoon.net reported that the platform handled $1.8 billion in trading volume over the past 30 days, an 80% increase over the prior 30-day period.

The numbers behind the surge

According to ComingSoon.net's summary of the Fortune report, Novig now ranks third among U.S.-regulated prediction market exchanges. It sits roughly $300 million ahead of fourth-place Crypto.com. Novig reportedly holds 3.5% of the total market. Kalshi leads with 64%, followed by Polymarket with 23%.

The outlet also reported that Novig's annualized gross revenue reached about $200 million in October, up from roughly $29 million in August. The company reportedly grew its staff from 20 to 100 employees this year.

The role of the "Just Sports" campaign

Sweeney starred in Novig's "Just Sports" advertising campaign, which launched in September. ComingSoon.net reported that first-time deposits rose 362% in the 30 days after the launch, and that mobile app downloads increased 261% over the same stretch. The app also briefly reached No. 1 on the App Store's top free sports apps chart, the outlet said.

Novig CEO Jacob Fortinsky told Fortune, as quoted by ComingSoon.net, "There are very few companies I think outside of AI that have achieved our level of growth in the span of two months."

Funding and background

Novig was founded in 2021. It operates as a sports-only, peer-to-peer prediction market for users 21 and older, and it launched as a federally regulated exchange in August. ComingSoon.net reported that the company raised a $75 million Series B in February at a $500 million valuation. The outlet added that Novig has reportedly reached a $2 billion valuation since the campaign began. Novig declined to comment on that figure, so it remains unconfirmed.

The campaign had already drawn wide attention online when it launched. The new figures give the first public look at what it may have meant for the company's business.

What's next

Novig has not announced further campaigns with Sweeney, and the valuation report is still unconfirmed. If the growth continues, the company's ranking among regulated exchanges and its next funding steps will show whether the "Just Sports" boost was lasting. For Sweeney, the numbers add to her profile as a business partner as well as an actress.

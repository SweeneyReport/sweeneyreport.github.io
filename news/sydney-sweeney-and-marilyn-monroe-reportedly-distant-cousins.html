Sydney Sweeney Is Reportedly Related To Marilyn Monroe

MyHeritage says new genealogy research links Sydney Sweeney and Marilyn Monroe as ninth cousins, one generation removed, through a 1600s settler.

By Sweeney Report · October 25, 2026

Sydney Sweeney and Marilyn Monroe are reportedly distant cousins. According to genealogy company MyHeritage, new research links the two actresses as ninth cousins, one generation removed. IMDb reported on the findings under the headline that the two are distant cousins, and Entertainment Weekly carried comments from a company executive. Sweeney had not publicly commented on the claim at the time of writing.

The shared ancestor

The link reportedly runs through Ezra Perry, a settler in Sandwich, Massachusetts, who lived in the 1600s. MyHeritage says both actresses descend from him, which is how it arrived at the ninth-cousin, once-removed relationship. In genealogy, the phrase means the two share a common ancestor several generations back, and that they sit at different generational distances from that person.

Because the connection goes back so many generations, it is a distant one. Relationships like this are common in large family trees, and they are typically established by tracing public records and family-tree data across centuries. The details of the underlying documents were not part of the coverage reviewed for this article.

Why the search began

Entertainment Weekly reported that MyHeritage's research director, Roi Mandel, said the search started after years of comparisons between Sweeney and Monroe. Sweeney has often been mentioned alongside the classic Hollywood star in media coverage and public conversation, and the company reportedly decided to look into whether the two were connected by family history.

The genealogy company's findings are its own research. They have not been confirmed by Sweeney or her representatives, so the claim should be treated as MyHeritage's conclusion rather than something verified by the actress.

Reaction so far

No response from Sweeney had been reported when the story was published. Neither IMDb nor Entertainment Weekly, as cited here, included a statement from her. Whether she addresses the finding in an interview or on social media remains to be seen.

What's next

The main open question is whether Sweeney responds to the report. Until then, the Monroe connection rests on MyHeritage's genealogy research, as relayed by Entertainment Weekly and IMDb. We will update this story if she or her team comment, or if MyHeritage publishes further details about the family tree behind the claim.

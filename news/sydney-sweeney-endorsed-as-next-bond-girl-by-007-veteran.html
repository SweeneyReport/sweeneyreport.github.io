Sydney Sweeney Endorsed as the Next Bond Girl by 007 Star

Trina Parks, the first Black Bond girl, told The Telegraph that Sydney Sweeney would be a great fit for Bond 26 as the franchise prepares a new 007.

By Sweeney Report · November 5, 2026

A woman who helped make Bond history has named her pick for the franchise's next leading lady. Trina Parks, who played the assassin Thumper in Diamonds Are Forever (1971), told The Telegraph that Sydney Sweeney would be a strong choice for a Bond girl in the upcoming Bond 26, according to Comic Book Resources and BGNES. Neither outlet reported any word of an approach to Sweeney.

Who is Trina Parks?

Parks, 79 according to BGNES, was the first Black actress to play a Bond girl. She appeared opposite Sean Connery's 007. Her endorsement therefore comes from someone who has worked inside the franchise.

What Parks said about Sweeney

Comic Book Resources quoted Parks as saying that Sweeney "would do a wonderful job." She pointed to the actor's range of roles and to what she called a classic Hollywood style and poise that suit Sweeney's projects and brand.

Parks also spoke about where the series is heading. She said modern audiences want the woman opposite Bond to be independent, and that the old pattern of women simply falling for him had run its course. BGNES noted that Parks made similar points in July, when she suggested Bond should have fewer romantic partners and that women in the films should be treated as equals.

The Bond 26 backdrop

The comments arrived during a busy week for Bond fans. Comic Book Resources reported that Callum Turner was expected to be announced as the next 007, with Denis Villeneuve directing for Amazon MGM Studios. The same outlet said attention would then turn to the casting of the main villain and the Bond girl.

BGNES, citing The Independent, reported earlier that several actors were screen-testing for the lead role. It named Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O'Connor, plus a fourth unidentified candidate. None of this has been officially confirmed.

Debbie McWilliams, a former Bond casting director, told The Independent that Bond must stay a mystery to audiences and that viewers must believe he is capable of killing. Ben Whishaw, who played Q, also said over the weekend that the franchise needs to be completely reinvented, BGNES reported.

What's next

An official announcement of Turner as Bond is expected soon, and the supporting roles will follow. For now, Parks's endorsement is one veteran's opinion rather than a casting report. Sweeney has not been linked to the film by any studio statement.

Sydney Sweeney's SYRN Remakes Christmas Classics for Holiday Ads

Sydney Sweeney's SYRN brand reportedly rolled out a holiday campaign in October that reimagines Christmas movies, with four collections planned.

By Sweeney Report · November 20, 2026

Sydney Sweeney's lingerie brand SYRN launched a holiday advertising campaign in October that draws on classic Christmas movies, according to the outlets covering it. Maxim reported that the ads reimagine festive favorites, including Home Alone and How the Grinch Stole Christmas. The campaign reportedly includes four collections, with the first one called Comfy.

The background

The holiday push follows an earlier SYRN campaign tied to Sweeney's birthday. That effort reportedly produced the brand's biggest sales day to date, though the company has not been quoted here with specific figures. The new campaign appears to build on that momentum by moving from a personal milestone to a seasonal theme that audiences already know well.

OK Magazine also covered the holiday campaign, publishing photos from the rollout. Its coverage focused on the images, while Maxim's report was the source for the film references used in this article.

The reactions

Coverage of the campaign has been mixed in tone. Some outlets framed it as nostalgic marketing that taps into the familiarity of holiday films. Others described the concept as edgy, reflecting how the brand has often courted attention with bold creative choices. No official statement explaining the creative strategy was cited in the reports reviewed.

Marketing observers often note that borrowing from well-loved films can draw quick recognition on social media, but it can also invite debate over how family-oriented properties are used in advertising. Those views were not attributed to a specific commentator in the coverage, so they should be read as general context rather than a reported reaction.

What it means for the brand

For Sweeney, SYRN is a business venture running alongside her acting career. A seasonal campaign built around recognizable titles gives the brand a clear hook during one of the busiest shopping periods of the year. Whether the approach lifts sales again has not been confirmed, and no results from the holiday collections had been reported at the time of writing.

What's next

What's next: Since four collections were reportedly planned and the first, Comfy, has already launched, more releases are expected as the season continues. Details on the remaining collections, and any sales results, had not been confirmed. The Sweeney Report will update this story as SYRN or Sweeney shares more.
